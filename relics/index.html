Kodanda

कोदण्डम्

WielderBhagavan Sri Rama
Cosmic ElementFire & Wind (Agni-Vayu)
Divine AttributeAbsolute Infallibility & Dharmic Precision
Scripture Invocation

चापमानय सौमित्रे शरांश्चाशीविषोपमान् । समुद्रं शोषयिष्यामि पद्भ्यां यान्तु प्लवङ्गमाः ॥

“Bring my bow, Lakshmana, and venomous arrows like serpents. I shall dry the ocean so the Vanaras may walk across on foot.”

Sacred Lore & Significance

The supreme longbow of Bhagavan Rama. Its resonant twang (Jyakara) sent shockwaves through the hearts of tyrannical forces across Bharatavarsha. In the hands of Rama, Kodanda shot with absolute moral rectitude — its arrows never struck the unarmed, the fleeing, or the penitent.
