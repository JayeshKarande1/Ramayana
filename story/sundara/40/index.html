Sarga 40

Valmiki Ramayana · Sundara Kanda · 24 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 40 / 68
1Sundara Kanda 40.1

श्रुत्वा तु वचनं तस्य वायुसूनोर्महात्मनः। उवाचात्महितं वाक्यं सीता सुरसुतोपमा।।5.40.1।।

śrutvā tu vacanṃ tasya vāyusūnormahātmanḥ| uvācātmahitṃ vākyṃ sītā surasutopamā||5.40.1||

Sita who was like the daughter of the gods heard the son of the Windgod for her benefit and replied:

2Sundara Kanda 40.2

त्वां दृष्ट्वा प्रियवक्तारं सम्प्रहृष्यामि वानरः। अर्धसञ्जातसस्येव वृष्टिं प्राप्य वसुन्धरा।।5.40.2।।

tvāṃ dṛṣṭvā priyavaktārṃ samprahṛṣyāmi vānarḥ| ardhasañjātasasyeva vṛṣṭiṃ prāpya vasundharā||5.40.2||

"O vanara just as the earth feels happy when the half grown crops receive rain, I feel happy after seeing you. You have been speaking pleasing words

3Sundara Kanda 40.3

यथा तं पुरुषव्याघ्रं गात्रैश्शोकाभिकर्शितैः। संस्पृशेयं सकामाहं तथा कुरु दयां मयि।।5.40.3।।

yathā tṃ puruṣavyāghrṃ gātraiśśokābhikarśitaiḥ| sṃspṛśeyṃ sakāmāhṃ tathā kuru dayāṃ mayi||5.40.3||

"Be kind to me and act in such a manner that I may embrace my lord passionately with my body emaciated with grief.

4Sundara Kanda 40.4

अभिज्ञानं च रामस्य दद्या हरिगणोत्तम। क्षिप्तामिषीकां काकस्य कोपादेकाक्षिशातनीम्।।5.40.4।।

abhijñānṃ ca rāmasya dadyā harigaṇottama| kṣiptāmiṣīkāṃ kākasya kopādekākṣiśātanīm||5.40.4||

"O best leader of vanaras remind Rama about the incident of the release of a blade of grass in anger blinding one of the eyes of the crow narrated to you.

5Sundara Kanda 40.5

मनश्शिलायास्तिलको गण्डपार्श्वे निवेशितः। त्वया प्रणष्टे तिलके तं किल स्मर्तुमर्हसि।।5.40.5।।

manaśśilāyāstilako gaṇḍapārśve niveśitḥ| tvayā praṇaṣṭe tilake tṃ kila smartumarhasi||5.40.5||

'(Further remind him), 'When my tilaka mark was erased, you painted tilaka with a stone (manassita pigment) nearby. O Rama you should remember that (incident).

6Sundara Kanda 40.6

स वीर्यवान्कथं सीतां हृतां समनुमन्यसे। वसन्तीं रक्षसां मध्ये महेन्द्रवरुणोपमः।।5.40.6।।

sa vīryavānkathṃ sītāṃ hṛtāṃ samanumanyase| vasantīṃ rakṣasāṃ madhye mahendravaruṇopamḥ||5.40.6||

'Rama you are like Indra and Varuna in valiance. How are you tolerating Sita seized, dwelling among the demons?

7Sundara Kanda 40.7

एष चूडामणिर्दिव्यो मया सुपरिरक्षितः। एतं दृष्ट्वा प्रहृष्यामि व्यसने त्वामिवानघ।।5.40.7।।

eṣa cūḍāmaṇirdivyo mayā suparirakṣitḥ| etṃ dṛṣṭvā prahṛṣyāmi vyasane tvāmivānagha||5.40.7||

"O sinless Rama this wonderful Chudamani of mine has been carefully safeguarded. Seeing this is like seeing you when I am in distress.

8Sundara Kanda 40.8

एष निर्यातितश्श्रीमान्मया ते वारिसम्भवः। अतः परं न शक्ष्यामि जीवितुं शोकलालसा।।5.40.8।।

eṣa niryātitaśśrīmānmayā te vārisambhavḥ| atḥ parṃ na śakṣyāmi jīvituṃ śokalālasā||5.40.8||

"I am sending you this (Chudamani) born of the sea. O glorious Rama and now engrossed in grief it is not possible for me to live.

9Sundara Kanda 40.9

असह्यानि च दुःखानि वाचश्च हृदयच्छिदः। राक्षसीनां सुघोराणां त्वत्कृते मर्षयाम्यहम्।।5.40.9।।

asahyāni ca duḥkhāni vācaśca hṛdayacchidḥ| rākṣasīnāṃ sughorāṇāṃ tvatkṛte marṣayāmyaham||5.40.9||

"I am tolerating this unbearable grief, and the words of the dreadful ogresses piercing my heart. Only for you.

10Sundara Kanda 40.10

धारयिष्यामि मासं तु जीवितं शत्रुसूदन। ऊर्ध्वं मासान्न जीविष्ये त्वया हीना नृपात्मज।।5.40.10।।

dhārayiṣyāmi māsṃ tu jīvitṃ śatrusūdana| ūrdhvṃ māsānna jīviṣye tvayā hīnā nṛpātmaja||5.40.10||

"O subduer of enemies O prince I will hold on to life only for a month. I will not survive beyond a month in your absence.

11Sundara Kanda 40.11

घोरो राक्षसराजोऽयं दृष्टिश्च न सुखा मयि। त्वां च श्रुत्वा विपद्यन्तं न जीवेयमहं क्षणम्।।5.40.11।।

ghoro rākṣasarājo'yṃ dṛṣṭiśca na sukhā mayi| tvāṃ ca śrutvā vipadyantṃ na jīveyamahṃ kṣaṇam||5.40.11||

"The demon king is frightful. He looks at me with immoral (lusty) eyes. I do not wish to live even for a moment after hearing about the adversities (you have faced)."

12Sundara Kanda 40.12

वैदेह्या वचनं श्रुत्वा करुणं साश्रु भाषितम्। अथाब्रवीन्महातेजा हनुमान्मारुतात्मजः।।5.40.12।।

vaidehyā vacanṃ śrutvā karuṇṃ sāśru bhāṣitam| athābravīnmahātejā hanumānmārutātmajḥ||5.40.12||

Hearing the words of Sita who was weeping piteously, brilliant Hanuman said this:

13Sundara Kanda 40.13

त्वच्छोकविमुखो रामो देवि सत्येन ते शपे। रामे दुःखाभिभूते तु लक्ष्मणः परितप्यते।।5.40.13।।

tvacchokavimukho rāmo devi satyena te śape| rāme duḥkhābhibhūte tu lakṣmaṇḥ paritapyate||5.40.13||

"O irreproachable lady I swear by you that Rama has grown averse to everything out of grief of separation from you. When Rama is in grief even Lakshmana gets immersed in sorrow.

14Sundara Kanda 40.14

कथञ्चिद्भवती दृष्टा न कालः परिशोचितुम्। इमं मुहूर्तं दुःखानामन्तं द्रक्ष्यसि भामिनि।।5.40.14।।

kathañcidbhavatī dṛṣṭā na kālḥ pariśocitum| imṃ muhūrtṃ duḥkhānāmantṃ drakṣyasi bhāmini||5.40.14||

"O beautiful lady somehow I could meet you. This is not the time to worry about. You will see the end of suffering at this very moment.

15Sundara Kanda 40.15

तावुभौ पुरुषव्याघ्रौ राजपुत्रावरिन्दमौ। त्वद्दर्शनकृतोत्साहौ लङ्कां भस्मीकरिष्यतः।।5.40.15।।

tāvubhau puruṣavyāghrau rājaputrāvarindamau| tvaddarśanakṛtotsāhau laṅkāṃ bhasmīkariṣyatḥ||5.40.15||

"Both the princes are tigers among men and crushers of enemies. They are determined to see you, and will reduce Lanka to ashes.

16Sundara Kanda 40.16

हत्त्वा च समरे क्रूरं रावणं सहबान्धवम्। राघवौ त्वां विशालाक्षि स्वां पुरीं प्रापयिष्यतः।।5.40.16।।

hattvā ca samare krūrṃ rāvaṇṃ sahabāndhavam| rāghavau tvāṃ viśālākṣi svāṃ purīṃ prāpayiṣyatḥ||5.40.16||

"O largeeyed lady Killing in war the vile Ravana, the demon king and all his relatives, Rama would take you back to his own city৷৷

17Sundara Kanda 40.17

यत्तु रामो विजानीयादभिज्ञानमनिन्दिते। प्रीतिसञ्जननं तस्य भूयस्त्वं दातुमर्हसि।।5.40.17।।

yattu rāmo vijānīyādabhijñānamanindite| prītisañjananṃ tasya bhūyastvṃ dātumarhasi||5.40.17||

"O unimpeachable lady give one more identification which would make him more happy".

18Sundara Kanda 40.18

साब्रवीद्दत्तमेवेति मयाभिज्ञानमुत्तमम्। एतदेव हि रामस्य दृष्ट्वा मत्केशभूषणम्।।5.40.18।। श्रद्धेयं हनुमन्वाक्यं तव वीर भविष्यति।

sābravīddattameveti mayābhijñānamuttamam| etadeva hi rāmasya dṛṣṭvā matkeśabhūṣaṇam||5.40.18|| śraddheyṃ hanumanvākyṃ tava vīra bhaviṣyati|

"I have already given the best identification. When you hand this jewel used on my hair to him, he will believe what you tell him".

19Sundara Kanda 40.19

स तं मणिवरं गृह्य श्रीमान्प्लवगसत्तमः।।5.40.19।। प्रणम्य शिरसा देवीं गमनायोपचक्रमे।

sa tṃ maṇivarṃ gṛhya śrīmānplavagasattamḥ||5.40.19|| praṇamya śirasā devīṃ gamanāyopacakrame|

The illustrious vanara held the ornament, prostrated to Sita and prepared to depart.

20Sundara Kanda 40.20

तमुत्पातकृतोत्साहमवेक्ष्य हरिपुङ्गवम्।।5.40.20।। वर्धमानं महावेगमुवाच जनकात्मजा। अश्रुपूर्णमुखी दीना बाष्पगद्गदया गिरा।।5.40.21।।

tamutpātakṛtotsāhamavekṣya haripuṅgavam||5.40.20|| vardhamānṃ mahāvegamuvāca janakātmajā| aśrupūrṇamukhī dīnā bāṣpagadgadayā girā||5.40.21||

Seeing Hanuman eager to leap, enlarging his body quickly, she appeared pitiable with her face filled with tears and throat choked.

22Sundara Kanda 40.22

हनुमन्सिंहसङ्काशौ भ्रातरौ रामलक्ष्मणौ। सुग्रीवं च सहामात्यं सर्वान् ब्रूयाह्यनामयम्।।5.40.22।।

hanumansiṃhasaṅkāśau bhrātarau rāmalakṣmaṇau| sugrīvṃ ca sahāmātyṃ sarvān brūyāhyanāmayam||5.40.22||

"O Hanuman to the lionlike brothers Rama and Lakshmana, to Sugriva including his ministers and to every one convey my good wishes.

23Sundara Kanda 40.23

यथा च स महाबाहुर्मां तारयति राघवः। अस्माद्दु:खाम्बुसम्रोधात्त्वं समाधातुमर्हसि।।5.40.23।।

yathā ca sa mahābāhurmāṃ tārayati rāghavḥ| asmāddu:khāmbusamrodhāttvṃ samādhātumarhasi||5.40.23||

"You should make efforts in such a manner that the mightyarmed Rama will help me in crossing the cean of sorrow.

24Sundara Kanda 40.24

इमं च तीव्रं मम शोकवेगं रक्षोभिरेभिः परिभर्त्सनं च। ब्रूयास्तु रामस्य गतस्समीपम् शिवश्च तेऽध्वाऽस्तु हरिप्रवीर।।5.40.24।।

imṃ ca tīvrṃ mama śokavegṃ rakṣobhirebhiḥ paribhartsanṃ ca| brūyāstu rāmasya gatassamīpam śivaśca te'dhvā'stu haripravīra||5.40.24||

"Foremost vanara tell Rama about my intense grief as well as the threats of the demons. May your journey be happy.

25Sundara Kanda 40.25

स राजपुत्र्य्राप्रतिवेदितार्थः कपिः कृतार्थः परिहृष्टचेताः। अल्पावशेषं प्रसमीक्ष्य कार्यं दिशं ह्युदीचीं मनसा जगाम।।5.40.25।।

sa rājaputryrāprativeditārthḥ kapiḥ kṛtārthḥ parihṛṣṭacetāḥ| alpāvaśeṣṃ prasamīkṣya kāryṃ diśṃ hyudīcīṃ manasā jagāma||5.40.25||

Hanuman understood the message of princess Sita and felt delighted at heart at his accomplishment. Considering the small task yet to be accomplished he reached the northern shore mentally (He thought of Rama who waited in Kishkinda which is in the northern direction). इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीय आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे चत्वारिंशस्सर्गः।। Thus ends the fortieth sarga of Sundarakanda of the holy Ramayana, the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
