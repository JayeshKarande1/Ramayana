Sarga 12

Valmiki Ramayana · Yuddha Kanda · 37 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 12 / 131
1Yuddha Kanda 12.1

सतांपरिषदंकृत्स्नांसमीक्ष्यसमितिञ्जयः । प्रचोदयामासतदाप्रहस्तंवाहिनीपतिम् ।।6.12.1।।

satāṃpariṣadṃkṛtsnāṃsamīkṣyasamitiñjayḥ | pracodayāmāsatadāprahastṃvāhinīpatim ||6.12.1||

Then Ravana who is victorious in wars, looking at the vast assembly (of Rakshasas) ordered Prahastha, the chief of the army.

2Yuddha Kanda 12.2

सेनापतेयथातेस्युःकृतविद्याश्चतुर्विधाः । योधानगररक्षायांतथाव्यादेष्टुमर्हसि ।।6.12.2।।

senāpateyathātesyuḥkṛtavidyāścaturvidhāḥ | yodhānagararakṣāyāṃtathāvyādeṣṭumarhasi ||6.12.2||

"O Chief of the army You order those who are well versed in the knowledge of all kinds of war and entrust those warriors of four divisions of the army to safeguard the city. Likewise, you ought to order them to be more alert."

3Yuddha Kanda 12.3

सप्रहस्तःप्रणीतात्माचिकीर्षन् राजशासनम् । विनिक्षिपद्बलंसर्वंबहिरन्तश्चमन्दिरे ।।6.12.3।।

saprahastḥpraṇītātmācikīrṣan rājaśāsanam | vinikṣipadbalṃsarvṃbahirantaścamandire ||6.12.3||

Celebrated Prahastha desiring to carry out the king's order assigned the army inside and outside the city.

4Yuddha Kanda 12.4

ततोविनिक्षिप्यबलंसर्वनगरगुप्तये । प्रहस्तःप्रमुखेराज्ञोनिषसादजगादच ।।6.12.4।।

tatovinikṣipyabalṃsarvanagaraguptaye | prahastḥpramukherājñoniṣasādajagādaca ||6.12.4||

There after Prahastha, having assigned the army for guarding the city, sat down in front of the king, and appealed.

5Yuddha Kanda 12.5

विहितंबहिरन्तश्चबलंबलवतस्तव । कुरुष्याविमनाःक्षिप्रंयदभिप्रेतमस्तुते ।।6.12.5।।

vihitṃbahirantaścabalṃbalavatastava | kuruṣyāvimanāḥkṣiprṃyadabhipretamastute ||6.12.5||

"You are a powerful one. Your army has been posted outside as well as inside. You may tell me at once without any distraction what you intend to do."

6Yuddha Kanda 12.6

प्रहस्तस्यवचश्श्रुत्वाराजाराज्यहितैषिणः । सुखेप्सुस्सुहृदांमध्येव्याजहारसरावणः ।।6.12.6।।

prahastasyavacaśśrutvārājārājyahitaiṣiṇḥ | sukhepsussuhṛdāṃmadhyevyājahārasarāvaṇḥ ||6.12.6||

Ravana who was keen on doing good to the kingdom desiring happiness and having heard Prahastha's word, said this in the midst of his dear ones.

7Yuddha Kanda 12.7

प्रियाप्रियेसुखंदुःखंलाभालाभेहिताहिते । धर्मकामार्थकृच्छ्रेषुयूयमर्हथवेदितुम् ।।6.12.7।।

priyāpriyesukhṃduḥkhṃlābhālābhehitāhite | dharmakāmārthakṛcchreṣuyūyamarhathaveditum ||6.12.7||

"When there is doubt about dharma, artha and kama, you are capable of deciphering between desirable and undesirable, happiness and sorrow, gain and loss."

8Yuddha Kanda 12.8

सर्वकृत्यानियुष्माभिस्समारब्धानिसर्वथा । मन्त्रकर्मनियुक्तानिनजातुविफलानिमे ।।6.12.8।।

sarvakṛtyāniyuṣmābhissamārabdhānisarvathā | mantrakarmaniyuktāninajātuviphalānime ||6.12.8||

"The actions carried out by me are always after it is well thought out and decided by all of you. I know that those actions have not failed."

9Yuddha Kanda 12.9

ससोमग्रहनक्षत्रैर्मरुद्भिरिववासवः । भवद्भिरहमत्यर्थंवृतश्रियमवाप्नुयाम् ।।6.12.9।।

sasomagrahanakṣatrairmarudbhirivavāsavḥ | bhavadbhirahamatyarthṃvṛtaśriyamavāpnuyām ||6.12.9||

"Just as Indra who rules over the moon, sun, stars, and gods attains fortune, accompanied by you I will attain fortune exceedingly at once."

10Yuddha Kanda 12.10

अहंतुखलुसर्वान्वस्समर्थयितुमुद्यतः । कुम्भकर्णस्यतुस्वप्नान्नेममर्थमचोदयम् ।।6.12.10।।

ahṃtukhalusarvānvassamarthayitumudyatḥ | kumbhakarṇasyatusvapnānnemamarthamacodayam ||6.12.10||

"Indeed, rightly I told all of you earlier. Since Kumbhakarna was sleeping I had not informed him about this matter."

11Yuddha Kanda 12.11

अयंहिसुप्तःषण्मासान्कुम्भकर्णोमहाबलः । सर्वशस्त्रभृतांमुख्यस्सइदानींसमुत्थितः ।।6.12.11।।

ayṃhisuptḥṣaṇmāsānkumbhakarṇomahābalḥ | sarvaśastrabhṛtāṃmukhyassaidānīṃsamutthitḥ ||6.12.11||

"Kumbhakarna, the foremost among the wielders of weapons, is very strong. He was sleeping these six months and has arrived now."

12Yuddha Kanda 12.12

इयंचदण्डकारण्याद्रामस्यमहिषीमया । रक्षोभिश्चरितोद्देशादानीताजनकात्मजा ।।6.12.12।।

iyṃcadaṇḍakāraṇyādrāmasyamahiṣīmayā | rakṣobhiścaritoddeśādānītājanakātmajā ||6.12.12||

"This Sita, the consort of Rama was brought from D and akaranya, a place where rakshasas wander."

13Yuddha Kanda 12.13

सामेवशय्यामारोढुमिच्छत्यलसगामिनी । त्रिषुलोकेषुचान्यामेनसीतासदृशीमता ।।6.12.13।।

sāmevaśayyāmāroḍhumicchatyalasagāminī | triṣulokeṣucānyāmenasītāsadṛśīmatā ||6.12.13||

"Sita who walks modestly did not like to enter into my bed (love me ) however much I prayed. I do not think there is anyone who looks like her in the three worlds."

14Yuddha Kanda 12.14

तनुमध्यापृथुश्रोणीशरदिन्दुनिभानना । हेमबिम्बनिभासौम्यामायेवमयनिर्मिता ।।6.12.14।।

tanumadhyāpṛthuśroṇīśaradindunibhānanā | hemabimbanibhāsaumyāmāyevamayanirmitā ||6.12.14||

"She is a woman of slender waist, plump hips, whose face is like an autumnal full moon. She is gentle and like a golden image. She is like a magical one constructed by Maya."

15Yuddha Kanda 12.15

सुलोहिततलौश्लक्ष् णौचरणौसुप्रतिष्ठितौ । दृष्ट्वाताम्रनखौतस्यादीप्यतेमेशरीरजः ।।6.12.15।।

sulohitatalauślakṣ ṇaucaraṇausupratiṣṭhitau | dṛṣṭvātāmranakhautasyādīpyatemeśarīrajḥ ||6.12.15||

"Beholding her smooth red soles and evenly shaped pinkish nails of feet, love is kindled in me."

16Yuddha Kanda 12.16

हुताग्निरर्चिस्सङ्काशामेनांसौरीमिवप्रभाम् । उन्नसंविमलंवल्गुविपुलांचारुलोचनम् ।।6.12.16।। पश्यंस्तदवशस्तस्याःकामस्यवशमेयिवान् ।

hutāgnirarcissaṅkāśāmenāṃsaurīmivaprabhām | unnasṃvimalṃvalguvipulāṃcārulocanam ||6.12.16|| paśyṃstadavaśastasyāḥkāmasyavaśameyivān |

"On beholding the sacrificial firelike face of Sita glowing like the radiance of the Sun, seeing her prominent nose, her large attractive countenance with beautiful eyes surely, I am overtaken by love and have become a slave to her."

17Yuddha Kanda 12.17

क्रोधहर्षसमानेनदुर्वर्णकरणेनच ।।6.12.17।। शोकसन्तापनित्येनकामेनकलुषीकृतः ।

krodhaharṣasamānenadurvarṇakaraṇenaca ||6.12.17|| śokasantāpanityenakāmenakaluṣīkṛtḥ |

"I have been disturbed by passion, which is equal in anger or happiness and makes one pale in grief or agony."

18Yuddha Kanda 12.18

सातुसंवत्सरंकालंमामयाचतभामिनी ।।6.12.18।। प्रतीक्षमाणाभर्तारंराममायतलोचना । तन्मयाचारुनेत्रायःप्रतिज्ञातंवचश्शुभम् ।।6.12.19।। श्रान्तोऽहंसततंकामाद्यातोहयइवाध्वनि ।

sātusṃvatsarṃkālṃmāmayācatabhāminī ||6.12.18|| pratīkṣamāṇābhartārṃrāmamāyatalocanā | tanmayācārunetrāyḥpratijñātṃvacaśśubham ||6.12.19|| śrānto'hṃsatatṃkāmādyātohayaivādhvani |

"Broadeyed Sita requested me one year time expecting her husband Rama to come (this is a false statement made by Ravana here) and I promised in turn to Sita of beautiful eyes. Just like an exhausted horse that marched I am tired on account of my passion."

20Yuddha Kanda 12.20

कथंसागरमक्षोभ्यंतरिष्यन्तिवनौकसः ।।6.12.20।। बहुसत्त्वसमाकीर्णंतौवादशरथात्मजौ ।

kathṃsāgaramakṣobhyṃtariṣyantivanaukasḥ ||6.12.20|| bahusattvasamākīrṇṃtauvādaśarathātmajau |

"How will the Vanaras and even both the sons of Dasaratha be able to cross the unassailable ocean inhabited by huge crocodiles?"

21Yuddha Kanda 12.21

अथवाकपिनैकेनकृतंनःकदनंमहत् ।।6.12.21।। दुर् ज्ञेयाःकार्यगतयोब्रूतयस्ययथामति ।

athavākapinaikenakṛtṃnḥkadanṃmahat ||6.12.21|| dur jñeyāḥkāryagatayobrūtayasyayathāmati |

"Even so, a huge destruction was done to us, by a single monkey. The consequences of their acts are unpredictable. Tell me what is there in your mind, according to your own understanding."

22Yuddha Kanda 12.22

मानुष्यान्नोभयंनास्तितथापितुविमृश्यताम् ।।6.12.22।। तदादेवासुरेयुद्धेयुष्माभिस्सहितोऽजयम् । तेमेभवन्तश्चतथासुग्रीवप्रमुखान्हरीन् ।।6.12.23।। परेपारेसमुद्रस्यपुरस्कृत्यनृपात्मजौ । सीतायाःपदवींप्राप्यसम्प्राप्तौवरुणालयाम् ।।6.12.24।।

mānuṣyānnobhayṃnāstitathāpituvimṛśyatām ||6.12.22|| tadādevāsureyuddheyuṣmābhissahito'jayam | temebhavantaścatathāsugrīvapramukhānharīn ||6.12.23|| parepāresamudrasyapuraskṛtyanṛpātmajau | sītāyāḥpadavīṃprāpyasamprāptauvaruṇālayām ||6.12.24||

"There is no fear of human beings for me. I won the war between Devas and asuras along with you. As it is, all of you are there. There at the shore of the ocean Rama and Lakshmana have collected with Sugriva and other leaders placing Vanaras in front to get back Sita. You may suggest a way out to gain."

25Yuddha Kanda 12.25

अदेयाचयथासीतावध्यौदशरथात्मजौ । भवद्भिर्मन्त्र्यतांमन्त्रस्सुनीतंचाभिधीयताम् ।।6.12.25।।

adeyācayathāsītāvadhyaudaśarathātmajau | bhavadbhirmantryatāṃmantrassunītṃcābhidhīyatām ||6.12.25||

"You may also think over and suggest to me a plan to kill Rama and Lakshmana without giving Sita."

26Yuddha Kanda 12.26

नहिशक्तिंप्रपश्यामिजगत्यन्यस्यकस्यचित् । सागरंवानरैस्तीर्वानिश्चयेनजयोमम ।।6.12.26।।

nahiśaktiṃprapaśyāmijagatyanyasyakasyacit | sāgarṃvānaraistīrvāniścayenajayomama ||6.12.26||

"No one can cross the ocean with Vanaras (help). As such I do not find anyone has the capacity. I am certain of victory."

27Yuddha Kanda 12.27

तस्यकामपरीतस्यनिशम्यपरिदेवितम् । कुम्भकर्णःप्रचुक्रोधवचनंचेदमब्रवीत् ।।6.12.27।।

tasyakāmaparītasyaniśamyaparidevitam | kumbhakarṇḥpracukrodhavacanṃcedamabravīt ||6.12.27||

On hearing the lamentation of Ravana seized with passion, enraged Kumbhakarna uttered these words.

28Yuddha Kanda 12.28

यदातुरामस्यसलक्ष्मणस्यप्रसह्यसीताखलुसाइहाऽहृता । सकृत्समीक्ष्यैवसुनिश्चितंतदाभजेतचित्तंयमुनेवयामुनम् ।।6.12.28।।

yadāturāmasyasalakṣmaṇasyaprasahyasītākhalusāihā'hṛtā | sakṛtsamīkṣyaivasuniścitṃtadābhajetacittṃyamunevayāmunam ||6.12.28||

"If you had at that time, that is before bringing Sita by force, which was accompanied by Rama and Lakshmana, discussed, and thought of, then you would be sure in your mind like the Yamuna River filling the Yamuna (mountain )."

29Yuddha Kanda 12.29

सर्वमेतन्महाराजकृतमप्रतिमंतव । विधीयेतसहास्माभिरादावेवास्यकर्मणः ।।6.12.29।।

sarvametanmahārājakṛtamapratimṃtava | vidhīyetasahāsmābhirādāvevāsyakarmaṇḥ ||6.12.29||

"Great king If you had discussed with us about all this action earlier, it would have been very good."

30Yuddha Kanda 12.30

न्यायेनराजकार्याणियःकरोतिदशानन: । नससन्तप्यतेपश्चान्निश्चितार्थमतिर्नृपः ।।6.12.30।।

nyāyenarājakāryāṇiyḥkarotidaśānana: | nasasantapyatepaścānniścitārthamatirnṛpḥ ||6.12.30||

"O Ravana Whoever is the king, having a sense of determination, thinks justifiably and carries out princely duties will not regret."

31Yuddha Kanda 12.31

अनुपायेनकर्माणिविपरीतानियानिच । क्रियमाणानिदुष्यन्तिहवींष्यप्रयतेष्विव ।।6.12.31।।

anupāyenakarmāṇiviparītāniyānica | kriyamāṇāniduṣyantihavīṃṣyaprayateṣviva ||6.12.31||

"Tasks carried out without proper plan and contrary to the right principles are spoilt like inauspicious oblations offered into sacrificial fire."

32Yuddha Kanda 12.32

यःपश्चात्पूर्वकार्याणिकर्माण्यभिचिकीर्षति । पूर्वंचापरकार्याणिनसवेदनयानयौ ।।6.12.32।।

yḥpaścātpūrvakāryāṇikarmāṇyabhicikīrṣati | pūrvṃcāparakāryāṇinasavedanayānayau ||6.12.32||

"He who seeks to perform earlier what has to be done later and does later what have to be done earlier is not a knower of right and wrong."

33Yuddha Kanda 12.33

चपलस्यतुकृत्येषुप्रसमीक्ष्याधिकंबलम् । क्षिप्रमन्येप्रपद्यन्तेक्रौञ्चस्यखमिवद्विजाः ।।6.12.33।।

capalasyatukṛtyeṣuprasamīkṣyādhikṃbalam | kṣipramanyeprapadyantekrauñcasyakhamivadvijāḥ ||6.12.33||

A hasty one who acts without thinking of the superior strength (of enemy) gets out at once like the swan penetrates into the crevice of Krauncha (mountain).

34Yuddha Kanda 12.34

त्वयेदंमहादारब्धंकार्यमप्रतिचिन्तितम् । दिष्ट्यात्वांनावधीद्रामोविषमिश्रमिवामिषम् ।।6.12.34।।

tvayedṃmahādārabdhṃkāryamapraticintitam | diṣṭyātvāṃnāvadhīdrāmoviṣamiśramivāmiṣam ||6.12.34||

"Without prior thought this great act has been done by you. Rama has not killed you, fortunately like poison mixed flesh would kill."

35Yuddha Kanda 12.35

तस्मात्त्वयासमारब्धंकर्मह्यप्रतिमंपरैः । अहंसमीकरिष्यामिहत्वाशत्रूंस्तवानघ ।।6.12.35।।

tasmāttvayāsamārabdhṃkarmahyapratimṃparaiḥ | ahṃsamīkariṣyāmihatvāśatrūṃstavānagha ||6.12.35||

"O Sinless Ravana Therefore, that which you have done is not possible for none other than me. I will kill your enemy and accomplish your goal for you."

36Yuddha Kanda 12.36

अहमुत्सादयिष्यामिशत्रूंस्तवनिशाचर । यदिशक्रविवस्वन्तौयदिपावकमारुतौ ।।6.12.36।। तावहंयोधयिष्यामिकुबेरवरुणावपि ।

ahamutsādayiṣyāmiśatrūṃstavaniśācara | yadiśakravivasvantauyadipāvakamārutau ||6.12.36|| tāvahṃyodhayiṣyāmikuberavaruṇāvapi |

"O Rakshasa I will ruin the enemy. I will fight with both of them (Rama and Lakshmana) even if they are Indra and sun, fire god and wind god or Kubera and Varuna."

37Yuddha Kanda 12.37

गिरिमात्रशरीरस्यमहापरिघयोधिनः । नर्दतस्तीक्ष्णदंष्ट्रस्यबिभीयाद्वैपुरन्दरः ।।6.12.37।।

girimātraśarīrasyamahāparighayodhinḥ | nardatastīkṣṇadṃṣṭrasyabibhīyādvaipurandarḥ ||6.12.37||

"If I go with a mountain like body and sharp teeth, holding a huge iron bar roaring like a lion, even Indra will run away out of fear."

38Yuddha Kanda 12.38

पुनर्मांसद्वितीयेनशरेणनिहनिष्यति । ततोऽहंतस्यपास्यामिरुधिरंकाममाश्वस ।।6.12.38।।

punarmāṃsadvitīyenaśareṇanihaniṣyati | tato'hṃtasyapāsyāmirudhirṃkāmamāśvasa ||6.12.38||

"By the time he releases a second arrow again, I will immediately drink his blood. Stay quiet with confidence."

39Yuddha Kanda 12.39

वधेनवैदाशरथेस्सुखावहंजयंतवाहर्तुमहंयतिष्ये । हत्वाचरामंसहलक्ष्मणेनखादामिसर्वान्हरियूथमुख्यान् ।।6.12.39।।

vadhenavaidāśarathessukhāvahṃjayṃtavāhartumahṃyatiṣye | hatvācarāmṃsahalakṣmaṇenakhādāmisarvānhariyūthamukhyān ||6.12.39||

"By killing Rama, I will try to make you happy and victorious. On killing Rama and Lakshmana like that I will consume the Vanara leaders."

40Yuddha Kanda 12.40

रमस्वकामम् पिबचाग्य्रवारुणींकुरष्वकार्याणिहितानिविज्वरः । मयातुरामेगमितेयमक्षयंचिरायसीतावशगाभविष्यति ।।6.12.40।।

ramasvakāmam pibacāgyravāruṇīṃkuraṣvakāryāṇihitānivijvarḥ | mayāturāmegamiteyamakṣayṃcirāyasītāvaśagābhaviṣyati ||6.12.40||

"You be free from fear, sporting and drinking the best of wines and doing actions that you like. Rama will be sent to the abode of the god of death. On reaching their Sita will be under your control for long." ।।इत्यार्षेवाल्मीकीयेश्रीमद्रामायणेआदिकाव्येयुद्धकाण्डेद्वादशस्सर्गः।। This is the end of twelfth sarga of Yuddha Kanda of the first epic the holy Ramayana composed by sage Valmiki.
