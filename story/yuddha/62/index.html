Yuddha Kanda•Sarga 62

Sarga 62

Valmiki Ramayana · Yuddha Kanda · 22 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 62 / 131
1Yuddha Kanda 62.1

स तुराक्षसशार्दूलोनिद्रामदसमाकुलः । राजमार्गंश्रियाजुष्टंययौविपुलविक्रमः ।।6.62.1।।

sa turākṣasaśārdūlonidrāmadasamākulḥ | rājamārgṃśriyājuṣṭṃyayauvipulavikramḥ ||6.62.1||

He (Kumbhakaran), who was endowed with indomitable prowess, a tiger among Rakshasas, was sleepy and inebriated, marched along the splendid royal path.

2Yuddha Kanda 62.2

राक्षसानांसहस्रैश्चवृतःपरमदुर्जयः । गृहेभ्यःपुष्पवर्षेणकीर्यमाणस्तदाययौ ।।6.62.2।।

rākṣasānāṃsahasraiścavṛtḥparamadurjayḥ | gṛhebhyḥpuṣpavarṣeṇakīryamāṇastadāyayau ||6.62.2||

Then, Kumbhakarna, who is very difficult to win over, walked violently surrounded by Rakshasas, covered with showers of flowers from houses.

3Yuddha Kanda 62.3

स हेमजालविततंभानुभास्वरदर्शनम् । ददर्शविपुलंरम्यंराक्षसेन्द्रनिवेशनम् ।।6.62.3।।

sa hemajālavitatṃbhānubhāsvaradarśanam | dadarśavipulṃramyṃrākṣasendraniveśanam ||6.62.3||

He saw the vast abode of the king of Rakshasas, with lattice work of gold and looking bright like the morning Sun

4Yuddha Kanda 62.4

स तत्तदासूर्यइवाभ्रजालंप्रविश्यरक्षोधिपतेर्निवेशम् । ददर्शदूरेऽग्रजमासनस्थंस्वयम्भुवंशक्रवासनस्थम् ।।6.62.4।।

sa tattadāsūryaivābhrajālṃpraviśyarakṣodhipaterniveśam | dadarśadūre'grajamāsanasthṃsvayambhuvṃśakravāsanastham ||6.62.4||

He saw entering the abode of Lord of Rakshasas like the Sun penetrating into a mass of clouds, from a distance his elder brother seated on the throne just like Indra saw the SelfBorn Brahma on the throne.

5Yuddha Kanda 62.5

भ्रातुस्सभवनंगच्छन्रक्षोगणसमन्विम् । कुम्भकर्णःपदन्यासैरकम्पयतमेदिनीम् ।।6.62.5।।

bhrātussabhavanṃgacchanrakṣogaṇasamanvim | kumbhakarṇḥpadanyāsairakampayatamedinīm ||6.62.5||

While Kumbhakarna accompanied by troops of Rakshasas, was taking strides to his brother's abode, even the earth was shaken.

6Yuddha Kanda 62.6

सोऽभिगम्यगृहंभ्रातुःकक्ष्यामभिविगाह्य च । ददर्शोद्विगन्नमासीनंविमानेपुष्पकेगुरुम् ।।6.62.6।।

so'bhigamyagṛhṃbhrātuḥkakṣyāmabhivigāhya ca | dadarśodvigannamāsīnṃvimānepuṣpakegurum ||6.62.6||

Having gone to his brother's residence, stepping into the enclosure, he saw his brother seated in a perturbed state.

7Yuddha Kanda 62.7

अथदृष्टवादशग्रीवःकुम्भकर्णमुपस्थितम् । तूर्णमुत्थायसंहृष्टःसन्निकर्षमुपानयत् ।।6.62.7।।

athadṛṣṭavādaśagrīvḥkumbhakarṇamupasthitam | tūrṇamutthāyasṃhṛṣṭḥsannikarṣamupānayat ||6.62.7||

The ten headed Ravana seeing Kumbhakarna, was delighted and got up quickly and made him come close to him.

8Yuddha Kanda 62.8

अथासीनस्यपर्यङ्केकुम्भकर्णोमहाबलः । भ्रातुर्ववन्देचरणौकिंकृत्यमितिचाब्रवीत् ।।6.62.8।।

athāsīnasyaparyaṅkekumbhakarṇomahābalḥ | bhrāturvavandecaraṇaukiṃkṛtyamiticābravīt ||6.62.8||

Getting up from his seat, Kumbhakarna endowed with extraordinary might bowed down and clasped his brother's feet and asked him, what task he had to do?

9Yuddha Kanda 62.9

उत्पत्यचैनंमुदितोरावणःपरिषस्वजे । स भ्रात्रासम्परिष्वक्तोयथावच्छाभिनन्दितः ।।6.62.9।। कुम्भकर्णश्शुभंदिव्यंप्रतिपेदेवरासनम् ।

utpatyacainṃmuditorāvaṇḥpariṣasvaje | sa bhrātrāsampariṣvaktoyathāvacchābhinanditḥ ||6.62.9|| kumbhakarṇaśśubhṃdivyṃpratipedevarāsanam |

Getting up from his seat Ravana embraced and greeted Kumbhakarna. So also, he duly rejoiced and occupied the seat.

10Yuddha Kanda 62.10

स तदासनमाश्रित्यकुम्भकर्णोमहाबलः ।।6.62.10।। संरक्तनयनःक्रोधाद्रावणंवाक्यमब्रवीत् ।

sa tadāsanamāśrityakumbhakarṇomahābalḥ ||6.62.10|| sṃraktanayanḥkrodhādrāvaṇṃvākyamabravīt |

Mighty Kumbhakarna, duly took the seat. Enraged Ravana with blood shot eyes spoke these words.

11Yuddha Kanda 62.11

किमर्थमहमादृत्यत्वयाराजन्विबोधितः ।।6.62.11।। शंसकस्माद्भयंतेऽत्रकोऽद्यप्रेतोभविष्यति ।

kimarthamahamādṛtyatvayārājanvibodhitḥ ||6.62.11|| śṃsakasmādbhayṃte'trako'dyapretobhaviṣyati |

"O King! For what purpose have I been awakened by you? Whom are you fearing? I wish to know who is to be ceased in life?"

12Yuddha Kanda 62.12

भ्रातरंरावणःक्रुद्धंकुम्भकर्णमवस्थितम् ।।6.62.12।। ईषत्तुपरिवृत्ताभ्यांनेत्राभ्यांवाक्यमब्रवीत् ।

bhrātarṃrāvaṇḥkruddhṃkumbhakarṇamavasthitam ||6.62.12|| īṣattuparivṛttābhyāṃnetrābhyāṃvākyamabravīt |

Kumbhakarna, was overcome with anger, and asked his brother Ravana, who was seated and rolling his eyes in anger.

13Yuddha Kanda 62.13

अद्यतेसुमहान्कालश्शयानस्यमहाबल ।।6.62.13।। सुखितस्त्वं न जानीषेममरामकृतंभयम् ।

adyatesumahānkālaśśayānasyamahābala ||6.62.13|| sukhitastvṃ na jānīṣemamarāmakṛtṃbhayam |

"Oh! Kumbhakarna of extraordinary prowess! You were asleep for long happily. You do not know that I am afraid of Rama."

14Yuddha Kanda 62.14

एषदाशरथीरामःसुग्रीवसहितोबली ।।6.62.14।। समुद्रंसबलस्तीर्त्वामूलंनःपरिकृन्तति ।

eṣadāśarathīrāmḥsugrīvasahitobalī ||6.62.14|| samudrṃsabalastīrtvāmūlṃnḥparikṛntati |

"Rama, the son of Dasaratha accompanied by Sugriva, crossed the ocean with an army that was cutting our roots."

15Yuddha Kanda 62.15

हन्तपश्यस्वलङ्कायावनान्युपवनानि च ।।6.62.15।। सेतुनासुखमागम्यवानरैकार्णवीकृतम् ।

hantapaśyasvalaṅkāyāvanānyupavanāni ca ||6.62.15|| setunāsukhamāgamyavānaraikārṇavīkṛtam |

" Alas! You see how the Vanaras, arriving at Lanka, crossing the bridge have done away with groves and gardens."

16Yuddha Kanda 62.16

येराक्षसामुख्यतमाहतास्तेवानरैर्युधि ।।6.62.16।। वानराणांक्षयंयुद्धे न पश्यामिकदाचन । नचापिवानरायुद्धेजितपूर्वाःकदाचन ।।6.62.17।।

yerākṣasāmukhyatamāhatāstevānarairyudhi ||6.62.16|| vānarāṇāṃkṣayṃyuddhe na paśyāmikadācana | nacāpivānarāyuddhejitapūrvāḥkadācana ||6.62.17||

"Of the Rakshasas, the chiefs have been killed in war by Vanaras. But I did not see even one Vanara to have been killed in war. Vanaras have not failed to succeed in war."

18Yuddha Kanda 62.18

तदेतद्भयमुत्पन्नंत्रायस्वेमांमहबल । नाशयत्वमिमानद्यतदर्थंबोधितोभवान् ।।6.62.18।।

tadetadbhayamutpannṃtrāyasvemāṃmahabala | nāśayatvamimānadyatadarthṃbodhitobhavān ||6.62.18||

"Mighty strong I am, but such is the fear that has been created in me from them. You have been awakened, to destroy them. Reported Ravana."

19Yuddha Kanda 62.19

सर्वक्षपितकोशं च स त्वमभ्यवपद्यमाम् । त्रायस्वेमांपुरींलङ्कांबालवृद्धावशेषिताम् ।।6.62.19।।

sarvakṣapitakośṃ ca sa tvamabhyavapadyamām | trāyasvemāṃpurīṃlaṅkāṃbālavṛddhāvaśeṣitām ||6.62.19||

" All resources of mine are exhausted. These children and aged are only left in Lanka city, knowing that you will save the city of Lanka."

20Yuddha Kanda 62.20

भ्रातुरर्थेमहाबाहो कुरुकर्मसुदुष्करम् । मयैवंनोक्तपूर्वोहिकश्चिद्भ्रातः परन्तप ।।6.62.20।।

bhrāturarthemahābāho kurukarmasuduṣkaram | mayaivṃnoktapūrvohikaścidbhrātḥ parantapa ||6.62.20||

"O broad shouldered brother! Do this task which is difficult for others. O Scourge of enemies! I have not asked you before for such a task."

21Yuddha Kanda 62.21

त्वय्यस्तिममस्नेहःपरासम्भावना च मे । दैवासुरेषुयुद्धेषुबहुशोराक्षसर्षभ ।।6.62.21।। त्वयादेवाःप्रतिव्यूह्यनिर्जिताश्चासुरायुधि ।

tvayyastimamasnehḥparāsambhāvanā ca me | daivāsureṣuyuddheṣubahuśorākṣasarṣabha ||6.62.21|| tvayādevāḥprativyūhyanirjitāścāsurāyudhi |

"O Bull among Rakshasas! Being my brother, do this for me. I always have affection for you. Considering that you have encountered war with Devas and Asuras and won both Devas and Asuras, do this help."

22Yuddha Kanda 62.22

तदेतत्सर्वमातिष्ठवीर्यंभीमपराक्रम ।।6.62.22।। न हितेसर्वभूतेषुदृश्यतेसदृशोबली ।

tadetatsarvamātiṣṭhavīryṃbhīmaparākrama ||6.62.22|| na hitesarvabhūteṣudṛśyatesadṛśobalī |

"O Brother of fierce valour! Get up. I have not seen anyone stronger than you among all beings."

23Yuddha Kanda 62.23

कुरुष्वमेप्रियहितमेतदुत्तमंयथाप्रियंप्रियरणबान्धवप्रिय । स्वतेजसाविधमसपत्नवाहिनींशरद्घनंपवनइवोद्यतोमहान् ।।6.62.23।।

kuruṣvamepriyahitametaduttamṃyathāpriyṃpriyaraṇabāndhavapriya | svatejasāvidhamasapatnavāhinīṃśaradghanṃpavanaivodyatomahān ||6.62.23||

"You are a lover of wars. You are a lover of relations. You are always my dear well wisher. O self glorious one! Do this task of throwing out the torrent army just like wind throws rainy clouds." ।। इत्यार्षेवाल्मीकीयेश्रीमद्रामायणेआदिकाव्येयुद्धकाण्डेष्टितमस्सर्गः ।। This is the end of the sixty second sarga of Yuddha Kanda of the first epic the holy Ramayana composed by sage Valmiki.
