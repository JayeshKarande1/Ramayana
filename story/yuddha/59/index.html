Sarga 59

Valmiki Ramayana · Yuddha Kanda · 140 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 59 / 131
1Yuddha Kanda 59.1

तस्मिन्हतेराक्षससैन्यपालेप्लवङ्गमानामृषभेणयुद्धे । भीमयुधंसागरतुल्यवेगंविदुद्रुवेराक्षसराजसैन्यम् ।।6.59.1।।

tasminhaterākṣasasainyapāleplavaṅgamānāmṛṣabheṇayuddhe | bhīmayudhṃsāgaratulyavegṃvidudruverākṣasarājasainyam ||6.59.1||

Commander in chief of the Rakshasa army killed by Neela, the bull of Vanaras, the army Rakshasas with dreadful weapons which were like the fury of an ocean, ran.

2Yuddha Kanda 59.2

गत्वाऽथरक्षोधिपतेश्शशंसुस्सेनापतिंपावकसूनुशस्तम् । तच्छापितेषांवचनंनिशम्यरक्षोधिपःक्रोधवशंजगाम ।।6.59.2।।

gatvā'tharakṣodhipateśśaśṃsussenāpatiṃpāvakasūnuśastam | tacchāpiteṣāṃvacanṃniśamyarakṣodhipḥkrodhavaśṃjagāma ||6.59.2||

Having gone to the king of Rakshasas, the survivors said, "Prahastha is killed by the son of fire god." Thereafter, the king of Rakshasas fell prey to anger.

3Yuddha Kanda 59.3

सङ् ख्येप्रहस्तंनिहतंनिशम्यशोकार्दितःक्रोधपरीतचेताः । उवाचतान्राक्षसयूथमुख्यानिन्द्रोयथानिर्जरयोथमुख्यान् ।।6.59.3।।

saṅ khyeprahastṃnihatṃniśamyaśokārditḥkrodhaparītacetāḥ | uvācatānrākṣasayūthamukhyānindroyathānirjarayothamukhyān ||6.59.3||

Even as Rakshasas revealed about Prahastha's death, tormented by grief and anger Ravana spoke to Rakshasa troops just as Indra spoke to celestial troops.

4Yuddha Kanda 59.4

नावज्ञारिपवेकार्यायैरिन्द्रबलसूदनः । सूदितस्सैन्यपालोमेसानुयात्रस्सकुञ्जरः ।।6.59.4।।

nāvajñāripavekāryāyairindrabalasūdanḥ | sūditassainyapālomesānuyātrassakuñjarḥ ||6.59.4||

"No low opinion to be entertained with regard to an enemy by whom my commander in chief of the army was killed, who is capable of killing the army of Indra with his followers and elephants."

5Yuddha Kanda 59.5

सोऽहंरिपुविनाशायविजयायाविचारयन् । स्वयमेवगमिष्यामिरणशीर्षंतदद्भुतम् ।।6.59.5।।

so'hṃripuvināśāyavijayāyāvicārayan | svayamevagamiṣyāmiraṇaśīrṣṃtadadbhutam ||6.59.5||

"I shall go myself without thinking to that wonderful battle scene and put an end to the enemy and succeed."

6Yuddha Kanda 59.6

अद्यतद्वानरानीकंरामंचसहलक्ष्मणम् । निर्दहिष्यामिबाणौघैर्वनंदीप्स्सैरिवानगिभिः ।।6.59.6।।

adyatadvānarānīkṃrāmṃcasahalakṣmaṇam | nirdahiṣyāmibāṇaughairvanṃdīpssairivānagibhiḥ ||6.59.6||

"Now I will go and burn up that army of Vanaras along with Lakshmana and Rama with the stream of my arrows just as forest fire without the use of fire."

7Yuddha Kanda 59.7

सएवमुक्त्वाज्वलनप्रकाशंरथंतुरङ्गोत्तमराजयुक्तम् । प्रकाशमानंवपुषाज्वलन्तंसमारुरोहामरराजशत्रुः ।।6.59.7।।

saevamuktvājvalanaprakāśṃrathṃturaṅgottamarājayuktam | prakāśamānṃvapuṣājvalantṃsamārurohāmararājaśatruḥ ||6.59.7||

Ravana, the enemy of the king of Devatas, having spoken like that ascended a chariot yoked to the best of horses, shining by virtue of its form (golden) and luminous like fire.

8Yuddha Kanda 59.8

सशङ्खभेरीपणवप्रणादैरास्फोटितक्षेळ्वितसिंहनादैः । पुण्यैस्स्तवैश्चाप्यभिपूज्यमानस्तदाययौराक्षसराजमुख्य:।। 6.59.8।।

saśaṅkhabherīpaṇavapraṇādairāsphoṭitakṣeळvitasiṃhanādaiḥ | puṇyaisstavaiścāpyabhipūjyamānastadāyayaurākṣasarājamukhya:|| 6.59.8||

As the foremost king of the Rakshasas went forth, a blast of sounds of conchs, beat of drums and clapping of arms and the roaring sounds followed by the chanting of auspicious verses sung by the Rakshasas eulogizing and honouring the king highly.

9Yuddha Kanda 59.9

सशैलजीमून्तिकाशरूपैर्मांसादनैःपावकदीप्तनेत्रैः । बभौवृतोराक्षसराजमुख्योभूतैर्वृतोरुद्रइवासुरेशः ।।6.59.9।।

saśailajīmūntikāśarūpairmāṃsādanaiḥpāvakadīptanetraiḥ | babhauvṛtorākṣasarājamukhyobhūtairvṛtorudraivāsureśḥ ||6.59.9||

The Rakshasa king surrounded by Rakshasas who resembled mountains and clouds in form seemed like Rudra surrounded by asuras.

10Yuddha Kanda 59.10

ततोनगर्यास्सहसामहौजानिष्क्रम्यतद्वानरसैन्यमुग्रम् । महार्णवाभ्रस्तनितंददर्शसमुद्यतंपादपशैलहस्तम् ।।6.59.10।।

tatonagaryāssahasāmahaujāniṣkramyatadvānarasainyamugram | mahārṇavābhrastanitṃdadarśasamudyatṃpādapaśailahastam ||6.59.10||

Thereafter, Ravana possessed with great splendour stopped at the city and saw the army of Vanaras collected together looking like an ocean of clouds wielding trees and rocks.

11Yuddha Kanda 59.11

तद्राक्षसानीकमतिप्रचण्डमालोक्यरामोभुजगेन्द्रबाहुः । विभीषणंशस्त्रभृतांवरिष्ठमुवाचसेवानुगतःपृथुश्रीः ।।6.59.11।।

tadrākṣasānīkamatipracaṇḍamālokyarāmobhujagendrabāhuḥ | vibhīṣaṇṃśastrabhṛtāṃvariṣṭhamuvācasevānugatḥpṛthuśrīḥ ||6.59.11||

Rama, whose arms were like the king of serpents, shining with splendour, followed by army, seeing the army of Rakshasas exceedingly furious, spoke to Vibheeshana the foremost of the wielder of weapons.

12Yuddha Kanda 59.12

नानापताकध्वजशस्त्रजुष्टंप्रासासिशूलायुथवज्रजुष्टम् । कस्येदमक्षोभ्यमभीरुजुष्टंमहेन्द्रोपमनागजुष्टम् ।।6.59.12।।

nānāpatākadhvajaśastrajuṣṭṃprāsāsiśūlāyuthavajrajuṣṭam | kasyedamakṣobhyamabhīrujuṣṭṃmahendropamanāgajuṣṭam ||6.59.12||

"Under whose command is this unshakeable army with several kinds of flags, posts and weapons armed with missiles, tridents, swords spikes and darts, with elephants the size of Mahendra mountain"

13Yuddha Kanda 59.13

ततस्तुरामस्यनिशम्यवाक्यंविभीषणंश्शक्रसमानवीर्यः । शशंसरामस्यबलप्रवेगंमहात्मनांराक्षसपुङ्गवानाम् ।।6.59.13।।

tatasturāmasyaniśamyavākyṃvibhīṣaṇṃśśakrasamānavīryḥ | śaśṃsarāmasyabalapravegṃmahātmanāṃrākṣasapuṅgavānām ||6.59.13||

Thereupon, Vibheeshana, equal to Indra in valour, on hearing the great soul Rama, revealed about the Rakshasa army endowed with great strength.

14Yuddha Kanda 59.14

योऽसौगजस्कन्धगतोमहात्मानवोदितार्कोपमताम्रवक्त्रः । प्रकम्पयन्नागशिरोऽभ्युपैतिह्यकम्पनंत्वेनमवेहिराजन् ।।6.59.14।।

yo'saugajaskandhagatomahātmānavoditārkopamatāmravaktrḥ | prakampayannāgaśiro'bhyupaitihyakampanṃtvenamavehirājan ||6.59.14||

"O King! the one seated on the elephant, by whose weight the elephant head is shaking, whose coppery face is like the rising sun, a gigantic one is Akampana."

15Yuddha Kanda 59.15

योऽसौरथस्थोमृगराजकेतुर्दून्वस्थिन्दनुश्शक्रधनुःप्रकाशम् । करीवभात्युग्रविवृत्तदंष्ट्रस्सइन्द्रजिन्नामवरप्रधानः ।।6.59.15।।

yo'saurathasthomṛgarājaketurdūnvasthindanuśśakradhanuḥprakāśam | karīvabhātyugravivṛttadṃṣṭrassaindrajinnāmavarapradhānḥ ||6.59.15||

"The one on the chariot, distinguished by the sign of a lion, and making sound with his bow, splendid as Indra's bow with terrific teeth projected forward looks like an elephant, eminent by virtue of the boons bestowed is Indrajith by name."

16Yuddha Kanda 59.16

यश्चैषव्निध्यास्तमहेन्द्रकल्पोधन्वीरथस्थोऽतिरथोतिवीरः । विष्पारयन् चापमतुल्यमानंनाम्नातिकायोऽतिविवृद्धकायः ।।6.59.16।।

yaścaiṣavnidhyāstamahendrakalpodhanvīrathastho'tirathotivīrḥ | viṣpārayan cāpamatulyamānṃnāmnātikāyo'tivivṛddhakāyḥ ||6.59.16||

"He who resembles Vindhya Mountain in the west (where the sun sets) and the Mahendra mountain (in size) seated on the chariot, is a great chariotwarrior, a hero unequalled in size, wielding a bow whose body is grown out of size, who is coming in sight is by name Atikaya."

17Yuddha Kanda 59.17

योऽसौनवार्कोदितताम्रचक्षुरारुह्यघण्टानिनदप्रणादम् । गजंखरंगर्जतिवैमहात्मामहोदरोनामसएषःवीरः ।।6.59.17।।

yo'saunavārkoditatāmracakṣurāruhyaghaṇṭāninadapraṇādam | gajṃkharṃgarjativaimahātmāmahodaronāmasaeṣḥvīrḥ ||6.59.17||

"He whose eyes are coppery red in anger, who rides on elephant resembles the beating of bells, roars in harsh tone are a great courageous one by the name Mahodara."

18Yuddha Kanda 59.18

योऽसौहयंकाञ्चनचित्रभाण्डमारुह्यसन्ध्याभ्रगिरिप्रकाशम् । प्रासंसमुद्यम्यमरीचिनद्धंपिशाचएषोऽशनितुल्यवेगः ।।6.59.18।।

yo'sauhayṃkāñcanacitrabhāṇḍamāruhyasandhyābhragiriprakāśam | prāsṃsamudyamyamarīcinaddhṃpiśācaeṣo'śanitulyavegḥ ||6.59.18||

"He who is mounted on horses decorated with gold shining, like a mountain crowned with cloud, radiant as the Sun holding the shining yokes coming at high speed is by name Pisacha."

19Yuddha Kanda 59.19

यश्चैषशूलंनिशितंप्रगृह्यविद्युत्प्रभंकिङ्करवज्रवेगम् । वृषेन्द्रमास्थायगिरिप्रकाशमायातियोऽसौत्रिशिरायशस्वी ।।6.59.19।।

yaścaiṣaśūlṃniśitṃpragṛhyavidyutprabhṃkiṅkaravajravegam | vṛṣendramāsthāyagiriprakāśamāyātiyo'sautriśirāyaśasvī ||6.59.19||

"He who comes firmly shining like lightning, surpassing thunderbolt in speed, seizing a trident, riding a Lordly bull shining like a mountain is the famous Trisira."

20Yuddha Kanda 59.20

असौचजीमूतनिकाशरूपःकुम्भःपृथुव्यूढसुजातवक्षाः । समाहितःपन्नगराजकेतुर्विष्पारयन्यातिधनुर्विधून्वन् ।।6.59.20।।

asaucajīmūtanikāśarūpḥkumbhḥpṛthuvyūḍhasujātavakṣāḥ | samāhitḥpannagarājaketurviṣpārayanyātidhanurvidhūnvan ||6.59.20||

"There comes Kumbha whose form is like a cloud in form, whose chest is fleshy and broad. He is fully prepared, coming with a sign of the king of serpents (Vasuki) stretching his bow."

21Yuddha Kanda 59.21

यश्चैषजाम्बूनदवज्रजुष्टंदीप्तंसधूमंपरिघंप्रगृह्य । आयातिरक्षोबलकेतुभूतःसोऽसौनिकुम्भोऽद्भुतघोरकर्मा ।।6.59.21।।

yaścaiṣajāmbūnadavajrajuṣṭṃdīptṃsadhūmṃparighṃpragṛhya | āyātirakṣobalaketubhūtḥso'saunikumbho'dbhutaghorakarmā ||6.59.21||

"There comes Nikumbha, studded with gold and diamonds, shining and smoky, who is the chief of Rakshasa army seizing an iron club, is capable of wonderful and terrific deeds."

22Yuddha Kanda 59.22

यश्चैषचापासिशरौघजुष्टंपताकिनंपावकदीप्तरूपम् । रथंसमास्थायविभात्युदग्रोनरान्तकोऽसौनगशृङ्गयोधी ।।6.59.22।।

yaścaiṣacāpāsiśaraughajuṣṭṃpatākinṃpāvakadīptarūpam | rathṃsamāsthāyavibhātyudagronarāntako'saunagaśṛṅgayodhī ||6.59.22||

Again, comes Naranthaka, with multitude of swords and arrows, mounted on top of a chariot with flags, blazing like fire, who fights with mountain peaks.

23Yuddha Kanda 59.23

यश्चैषनानाविधघोररूपैर्व्याघ्रोष्ट्रनागेन्द्रमृगाश्ववक्त्रै: । भूतैर्वृतोभातिविवृत्तनेत्स्रैसोऽसौसुराणामपिदर्पहन्ता ।।6.59.23।। त्रैतदिन्दुप्रतिमंविभातिछत्रंसितंसूक्ष्मशलाकमग्य्रम् । अत्रैषरक्षोधिपतिर्महात्माभूतैर्वृतोरुद्रइवावभाति ।।6.59.24।।

yaścaiṣanānāvidhaghorarūpairvyāghroṣṭranāgendramṛgāśvavaktrai: | bhūtairvṛtobhātivivṛttanetsraiso'sausurāṇāmapidarpahantā ||6.59.23|| traitadindupratimṃvibhātichatrṃsitṃsūkṣmaśalākamagyram | atraiṣarakṣodhipatirmahātmābhūtairvṛtorudraivāvabhāti ||6.59.24||

"One who is surrounded by several hideous forms, like heads of tigers, camels, Lordly elephants and horses, rolling eyes, just as Rudra by his attendants, who is surrounded by Rakshasas and is humbled by the pride of even Devatas, over him shines the white canopy resembling the moon with slender ribs is the king of Rakshasas."

25Yuddha Kanda 59.25

असौकिरीटीचलकुण्डलास्योनगेन्द्रविन्ध्योपमभीमकायः । महेन्द्रवैवस्वतदर्पहन्तारक्षोधिपस्सूर्यइवावभाति ।।6.59.25।।

asaukirīṭīcalakuṇḍalāsyonagendravindhyopamabhīmakāyḥ | mahendravaivasvatadarpahantārakṣodhipassūryaivāvabhāti ||6.59.25||

"There is the king of Rakshasas shining like Sun, whose countenance shines with hanging earrings, of terrible form like the Himalayas and Vindhya, who has humbled the pride of Mahendra and Yama."

26Yuddha Kanda 59.26

प्रत्युवाचततोरामोविभीषणमरिन्दम् । अहोदीप्तमहातेजारावणोराक्षसेश्वरः ।।6.59.26।।

pratyuvācatatorāmovibhīṣaṇamarindam | ahodīptamahātejārāvaṇorākṣaseśvarḥ ||6.59.26||

Then Rama the scourge of enemies replied Vibheeshana, "Oh! Ravana! King of Rakshasas is endowed with luminous glory."

27Yuddha Kanda 59.27

आदित्यइवदुष्प्रेक्ष्योरश्मिभिर्भातिरावणः । सुव्यक्तंलक्ष्यतेह्यस्यरूपंतेजःसमावृतम् ।।6.59.27।।

ādityaivaduṣprekṣyoraśmibhirbhātirāvaṇḥ | suvyaktṃlakṣyatehyasyarūpṃtejḥsamāvṛtam ||6.59.27||

"Ravana blazing like the sun is difficult to gaze even. His shine is spread all over and not perceptible to discern."

28Yuddha Kanda 59.28

देवदानववीराणांवपुर्नैवंविधंभवेत् । यादृशंराक्षसेन्द्रस्यवपुरेतत्प्रकाशते ।।6.59.28।।

devadānavavīrāṇāṃvapurnaivṃvidhṃbhavet | yādṛśṃrākṣasendrasyavapuretatprakāśate ||6.59.28||

Among the Devas and Danavas such brilliance is not there. He shines so brightly.

29Yuddha Kanda 59.29

सर्वेपर्वतसङ्काशास्सर्वेपर्वतयोधिनः । सर्वेदीप्तायुधधरायोधाश्चास्यमहोजस:।। 6.59.29।।

sarveparvatasaṅkāśāssarveparvatayodhinḥ | sarvedīptāyudhadharāyodhāścāsyamahojasa:|| 6.59.29||

"The great warriors of Ravana fighting in war are like mountains and all fought with mountains and glowing weapons."

30Yuddha Kanda 59.30

भातिरक्षोराजोऽसौप्रदीप्त्तैर्भीमविक्रमैः । भूतैःपरिवृतस्तीक्ष्णैर्देहवद्भिरिवान्तकः ।।6.59.30।।

bhātirakṣorājo'saupradīpttairbhīmavikramaiḥ | bhūtaiḥparivṛtastīkṣṇairdehavadbhirivāntakḥ ||6.59.30||

"The Rakshasa king is surrounded by sharp, furious beings of terrible valour shining like the god of death."

31Yuddha Kanda 59.31

दिष्ट्याऽयमद्यपापात्माममदृष्टिपथंगतः । अद्यक्रोधंविमोक्ष्यामिसीताहरणसम्भवम् ।।6.59.31।।

diṣṭyā'yamadyapāpātmāmamadṛṣṭipathṃgatḥ | adyakrodhṃvimokṣyāmisītāharaṇasambhavam ||6.59.31||

"By his good luck, this sinful one has come under my sight. I will give vent to my wrath born of Sita's abduction."

32Yuddha Kanda 59.32

एवमुक्त्वाततोरामोधनुरादायवीर्यवान् । लक्ष्मणानुचरस्तस्थौसमुद्धृत्यशरोत्तमम् ।।6.59.32।।

evamuktvātatorāmodhanurādāyavīryavān | lakṣmaṇānucarastasthausamuddhṛtyaśarottamam ||6.59.32||

Indomitable Rama having spoken in that manner, followed by Lakshmana got ready and took out an excellent bow and stood.

33Yuddha Kanda 59.33

ततःसरक्षोधिपतिर्महात्मारक्षांसितान्याहमहाबलानि । द्वारेषुचर्यागृहगोपुरेषुसुनिर्वृतास्तिष्ठतनिर्विशङ्काः ।।6.59.33।।

tatḥsarakṣodhipatirmahātmārakṣāṃsitānyāhamahābalāni | dvāreṣucaryāgṛhagopureṣusunirvṛtāstiṣṭhatanirviśaṅkāḥ ||6.59.33||

There upon the great soul, the king of Rakshasas thus spoke to the strong army of Rakshasas, "Fully complacent, free from any apprehension be stationed at the gates and the buildings of the four main streets."

34Yuddha Kanda 59.34

हाऽगतंमांसहितंभवद्भिर्वनौकसश्छिद्रमिदंविदित्वा । शून्यापुरींदुष्प्रसहांप्रमथ्यप्रधर्षयेयुस्सहसासमेताः ।।6.59.34।।

hā'gatṃmāṃsahitṃbhavadbhirvanaukasaśchidramidṃviditvā | śūnyāpurīṃduṣprasahāṃpramathyapradharṣayeyussahasāsametāḥ ||6.59.34||

"Coming to know that I am here with you, this side, considering it to be a weak point, the forest dwellers may enter the vacant city and Devastate all united."

35Yuddha Kanda 59.35

विसर्जयित्वासचिवांस्ततस्तान्गतेषुरक्षस्सुयथानियोगम् । व्यचारयद्वानरसागरौघंमहाझषःपूर्णमिवार्णवौघम् ।।6.59.35।।

visarjayitvāsacivāṃstatastāngateṣurakṣassuyathāniyogam | vyacārayadvānarasāgaraughṃmahājhaṣḥpūrṇamivārṇavaugham ||6.59.35||

After the counsellors departed from there Ravana pierced through the ocean of Vanaras just as gigantic fish would pierce in the sea.

36Yuddha Kanda 59.36

तमापतन्तंसहसासमीक्ष्यदीप्तेषुचापंयुधिराक्षसेन्द्रम् । महत्समुत्पाट्यमहीधराग्रंदुद्रावरक्षोधिपतिंहरीशः ।।6.59.36।।

tamāpatantṃsahasāsamīkṣyadīpteṣucāpṃyudhirākṣasendram | mahatsamutpāṭyamahīdharāgrṃdudrāvarakṣodhipatiṃharīśḥ ||6.59.36||

Watching Ravana advancing suddenly on the battlefield with great speed, Sugriva took a huge mountain top and went towards Rakshasa king.

37Yuddha Kanda 59.37

तच्छैलशृङ्गंबहुवृक्षसानुंप्रगृह्यचिक्षेपनिशाचराय । तमापतन्तंसहसासमीक्ष्यचिच्छेदबाणैस्तपनीयपुङ्खैः ।।6.59.37।।

tacchailaśṛṅgṃbahuvṛkṣasānuṃpragṛhyacikṣepaniśācarāya | tamāpatantṃsahasāsamīkṣyacicchedabāṇaistapanīyapuṅkhaiḥ ||6.59.37||

Seizing mountain tops whose slopes were filled with trees Sugriva hurled at the night rangers. Watching this Ravana quickly broke them with his arrows fixed with golden shafts.

38Yuddha Kanda 59.38

तस्मिन् प्रवृद्धोत्तमसानुवृक्षेशृङ्गेविकीर्णेपतितेपृथिव्याम् । महाहिकल्पंशरमन्तकाभंसमादधेराक्षसलोकनाथः ।।6.59.38।।

tasmin pravṛddhottamasānuvṛkṣeśṛṅgevikīrṇepatitepṛthivyām | mahāhikalpṃśaramantakābhṃsamādadherākṣasalokanāthḥ ||6.59.38||

After that slope covered with excellent trees had fallen on the ground and broken, Ravana got readied with an arrow as if for arranging destruction like the spirit of death.

39Yuddha Kanda 59.39

सतंगृहीत्वाऽनिलतुल्यवेगंसविस्फुलिङ्गज्वलनप्रकाशम् । बाणंमहेन्द्राशनितुल्यवेगंचिक्षेपसुग्रीववधायरुष्टः ।।6.59.39।।

satṃgṛhītvā'nilatulyavegṃsavisphuliṅgajvalanaprakāśam | bāṇṃmahendrāśanitulyavegṃcikṣepasugrīvavadhāyaruṣṭḥ ||6.59.39||

Seizing an arrow which shone like sparks of fire and had the speed of wind, nay vied with thunderbolt of Indra in speed, he (Ravana) released an arrow in anger.

40Yuddha Kanda 59.40

ससायकोरावणबाहुमुक्तःशक्राशनिप्रख्यवपुश्शिताग्रः । सुग्रीवमासाद्यबिभेदवेगाद्गुहेरिताक्रौञ्चमिवोग्रशक्तिः ।।6.59.40।।

sasāyakorāvaṇabāhumuktḥśakrāśaniprakhyavapuśśitāgrḥ | sugrīvamāsādyabibhedavegādguheritākrauñcamivograśaktiḥ ||6.59.40||

That arrow released from Ravana's arm, which was hard and sharp as the thunderbolt of Indra, pierced Sugriva just as the sharp thorn edged spear of Guha penetrated Krauncha mountain.

41Yuddha Kanda 59.41

ससायकार्तोविपरीतचेताःकूजन्पृथिव्यांनिपपातवीरः । तंप्रेक्ष्यभूमौपतितंविसंज्ञंनेदुःप्रहृष्टायुधियातुधानाः ।।6.59.41।।

sasāyakārtoviparītacetāḥkūjanpṛthivyāṃnipapātavīrḥ | tṃprekṣyabhūmaupatitṃvisṃjñṃneduḥprahṛṣṭāyudhiyātudhānāḥ ||6.59.41||

That hero (Sugriva) hurt by the arrow lost senses and fell on the ground groaning in the battlefield. Seeing that the Rakshasas shouted joyfully overtaken by evil spirits.

42Yuddha Kanda 59.42

ततोगवाक्षोगवयस्सुदंष्ट्रस्त्वथर्षभोज्योतिमुखोनलश्च । शैलान् समुत्पाट्यविवृद्धकायाःप्रदुद्रुवुस्तंप्रतिराक्षसेन्द्रम् ।।6.59.42।।

tatogavākṣogavayassudṃṣṭrastvatharṣabhojyotimukhonalaśca | śailān samutpāṭyavivṛddhakāyāḥpradudruvustṃpratirākṣasendram ||6.59.42||

Thereafter Gavaksha, Gavaya, Sudamshtra, Rshaba, Jyothimukha and Nala increasing their body size rushed towards the king of Rakshasas.

43Yuddha Kanda 59.43

तेषांप्रहारान् सचकारमोघान् रक्षोधिपोबाणशतैःशिताग्रैः । तान्वानरेन्द्रानपिबाणजालैर्बिभेदजाम्बूनदचित्रपुङ्खैः ।।6.59.43।।

teṣāṃprahārān sacakāramoghān rakṣodhipobāṇaśataiḥśitāgraiḥ | tānvānarendrānapibāṇajālairbibhedajāmbūnadacitrapuṅkhaiḥ ||6.59.43||

The Rakshasa Lord made the mountain peaks useless by the hundreds of arrows released by him. The streams of arrows with golden shafts could resist the Rakshasas and strike the Vanara king.

44Yuddha Kanda 59.44

तेवानरेन्द्रास्त्रिदशारिबाणैर्भिन्नानिपेतुर्भुविभीमकायाः । ततस्तुतद्वानरसैन्यमुग्रंप्रच्छादयामाससबाणजालैः ।।6.59.44।।

tevānarendrāstridaśāribāṇairbhinnānipeturbhuvibhīmakāyāḥ | tatastutadvānarasainyamugrṃpracchādayāmāsasabāṇajālaiḥ ||6.59.44||

The Vanara leaders of fierce form torn by the streams of sharp arrows discharged in the three directions fell covering the ground.

45Yuddha Kanda 59.45

तेवध्यमानाःपतिताःप्रवीरानानद्यमानाभयशल्यविद्धाः । शाखामृगारावणसायकार्ताजग्मुश्शरण्यंशरणंस्मरामम् ।।6.59.45।।

tevadhyamānāḥpatitāḥpravīrānānadyamānābhayaśalyaviddhāḥ | śākhāmṛgārāvaṇasāyakārtājagmuśśaraṇyṃśaraṇṃsmarāmam ||6.59.45||

Those monkeys struck and fallen by the piercing of arrows shrieking went to seek refuge from Rama.

46Yuddha Kanda 59.46

ततोमहात्मासधनुर्धनुष्मानादायरामस्सहसाजगाम । तंलक्ष्मणःप्राञ्जलिरभ्युपेत्यउवाचवाक्यंपरमार्थयुक्तम् ।।6.59.46।।

tatomahātmāsadhanurdhanuṣmānādāyarāmassahasājagāma | tṃlakṣmaṇḥprāñjalirabhyupetyauvācavākyṃparamārthayuktam ||6.59.46||

The great soul Rama who was holding a bow went seizing it. Lakshmana approached him immediately with folded hands and addressed Rama with words consonant with truth.

47Yuddha Kanda 59.47

काममार्यस्सुपर्याप्तोवधायास्यदुरात्मनः । विधमिष्याम्यहंचैतमनुजानीहिमांविभो ।।6.59.47।।

kāmamāryassuparyāptovadhāyāsyadurātmanḥ | vidhamiṣyāmyahṃcaitamanujānīhimāṃvibho ||6.59.47||

"I will exterminate the evil minded Ravana. Oh! noble brother, may I be permitted to complete the task"

48Yuddha Kanda 59.48

तमब्रवीन्महातेजारामस्सत्यपराक्रमः । गच्छयत्नपरश्चापिभवलक्ष्मण संयुगे ।।6.59.48।।

tamabravīnmahātejārāmassatyaparākramḥ | gacchayatnaparaścāpibhavalakṣmaṇa sṃyuge ||6.59.48||

Sri Rama, who was highly energetic and truthful in enthusiasm to succeed, told Lakshmana, "You go and be careful in the battle."

49Yuddha Kanda 59.49

रावणोहिमहावीर्योरणेऽद्भुतपराक्रमः । त्रैलोक्येनापिसङ्क्रुद्धोदुष्प्रसह्योनसंशयः ।।6.59.49।।

rāvaṇohimahāvīryoraṇe'dbhutaparākramḥ | trailokyenāpisaṅkruddhoduṣprasahyonasṃśayḥ ||6.59.49||

"Ravana is a great hero in the battlefield. His valour in war is wonderful. He is angry but none can withstand him in the three worlds."

50Yuddha Kanda 59.50

तस्यच्छिद्राणिमार्गस्वस्वच्छिद्राणिचलक्ष्य । चक्षुषाधनुषायत्ना प्रक्षात्मानंसमाहितः ।।6.59.50।।

tasyacchidrāṇimārgasvasvacchidrāṇicalakṣya | cakṣuṣādhanuṣāyatnā prakṣātmānṃsamāhitḥ ||6.59.50||

"Remaining alert, apply your arrows in the direction of his arrows. Looking at his arrows carefully, making an effort to protect yourself, set your bow."

51Yuddha Kanda 59.51

राघवस्यवचश्श्रुत्वासपरिष्वज्याभिपूज्यच । अभिवाद्यतत् रामंययौसौमित्रिराहवम् ।।6.59.51।।

rāghavasyavacaśśrutvāsapariṣvajyābhipūjyaca | abhivādyatat rāmṃyayausaumitrirāhavam ||6.59.51||

On hearing Raghava's words, son of Sumitra embraced him and offered prayers, paying respects sallied forth for the encounter.

52Yuddha Kanda 59.52

सरावणंवारणहस्तबाहुंददर्शभीमोद्यतदीप्तचापम् । प्रच्छादयन्तंशरवृष्टिजालैस्तान्वानराभनिन्नविकीर्णदेहान् ।।6.59.52।।

sarāvaṇṃvāraṇahastabāhuṃdadarśabhīmodyatadīptacāpam | pracchādayantṃśaravṛṣṭijālaistānvānarābhaninnavikīrṇadehān ||6.59.52||

He saw Ravana, endowed with round shoulders like elephant trunks, with fierce bow glowing and the broken bodies of Vanaras spread all over. Ravana was showering nets of arrows and instigating vanaras.

53Yuddha Kanda 59.53

तमालोक्यमहातेजाहनूमान्मारुतात्मजः । निवार्यशरजालानिप्रदुद्रावसरावणम् ।।6.59.53।।

tamālokyamahātejāhanūmānmārutātmajḥ | nivāryaśarajālānipradudrāvasarāvaṇam ||6.59.53||

The son of the wind god, Hanuman, a brilliant one seeing Ravana, obstructed the stream of arrows and darted towards him.

54Yuddha Kanda 59.54

रथंतस्यसमासाद्यबाहुमुद्यम्यदक्षिणम् । त्रासयन्रावणंधीमान्हनूमान्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ।।6.59.54।।

rathṃtasyasamāsādyabāhumudyamyadakṣiṇam | trāsayanrāvaṇṃdhīmānhanūmānvākyamabravīt ||6.59.54||

Courageous Hanuman seeing the chariot of Ravana lifting his right arm menacing him spoke these words.

55Yuddha Kanda 59.55

देवदानवगन्धर्वायक्षाश्चसहराक्षैसैः । अवश्यंतुत्वयाभग्नावानरेभ्यस्तुतेभयम् ।।6.59.55।।

devadānavagandharvāyakṣāścasaharākṣaisaiḥ | avaśyṃtutvayābhagnāvānarebhyastutebhayam ||6.59.55||

"Your life is surely secure from gods, demons, Gandharvas and Yakshas and Rakshasas but you fear Vanaras."

56Yuddha Kanda 59.56

एषमेदक्षिणोबाहुःपञ्चशाखःसमुद्यतः । विधमिष्यतितेदेहाद्भूतात्मानंचिरोषितम् ।।6.59.56।।

eṣamedakṣiṇobāhuḥpañcaśākhḥsamudyatḥ | vidhamiṣyatitedehādbhūtātmānṃciroṣitam ||6.59.56||

"I raised this right arm that way with five branches (five fingers) at you that will expel your subtle self (made up of the five subtle elements) from your body."

57Yuddha Kanda 59.57

श्रुत्वाहनुमतोवाक्यंरावणोभीमविक्रमः । संरक्तनयनःक्रोधादिदंवचनमब्रवीत् ।।6.59.57।।

śrutvāhanumatovākyṃrāvaṇobhīmavikramḥ | sṃraktanayanḥkrodhādidṃvacanamabravīt ||6.59.57||

Ravana of truly fierce valour, hearing Hanuman's words, his eyes became blood red and spoke to Hanuman this way.

58Yuddha Kanda 59.58

क्षिप्रंप्रहरनिःशङ्कंस्थिरांकीर्तिमवाप्नुहि । ततस्त्वांज्ञातविक्रान्तंनाशयिष्यामिवानर ।।6.59.58।।

kṣiprṃpraharaniḥśaṅkṃsthirāṃkīrtimavāpnuhi | tatastvāṃjñātavikrāntṃnāśayiṣyāmivānara ||6.59.58||

"O Vanara! Go about without any hesitation. Attain the fame of a Vanara hitting Ravana permanently. Thereafter knowing your prowess, I shall put an end to you."

59Yuddha Kanda 59.59

रावणस्यवचश्श्रुत्वावायुसूनुर्वचोऽब्रवीत् । प्रहृतंहिमयापूर्वमक्षंतवसुतंतव ।।6.59.59।।

rāvaṇasyavacaśśrutvāvāyusūnurvaco'bravīt | prahṛtṃhimayāpūrvamakṣṃtavasutṃtava ||6.59.59||

Having heard Ravana's words, Hanuman said, "Think of your son Akshaya killed by me earlier."

60Yuddha Kanda 59.60

एवमुक्तोमहातेजारावणोराक्षसेश्वरः । आजघानानिलसुतंतलेनोरसिवीर्यवान् ।।6.59.60।।

evamuktomahātejārāvaṇorākṣaseśvarḥ | ājaghānānilasutṃtalenorasivīryavān ||6.59.60||

Having spoken that way by Hanuman, the Lord of Rakshasas slapped his hand on Hanuman's chest.

61Yuddha Kanda 59.61

सतलाभिहतस्तेनचचालचमुहुर्मुहुः । स्थित्वामुहूर्तंतेजस्वीस्थैर्यंकृत्वामहामतिः ।।6.59.61।। आजघानाभिसङ्कृद्धस्तलेनैवामरद्विषम् ।

satalābhihatastenacacālacamuhurmuhuḥ | sthitvāmuhūrtṃtejasvīsthairyṃkṛtvāmahāmatiḥ ||6.59.61|| ājaghānābhisaṅkṛddhastalenaivāmaradviṣam |

Smacked by him (Ravana), Hanuman, a brilliant and intelligent one stood firm for a while and picking up courage, highly enraged struck the enemy of gods with his palm.

62Yuddha Kanda 59.62

ततस्ततेनाभिहतोवानरेणमहात्मना ।।6.59.62।। दशग्रीवःसमाधूतोयथाभूमिचलेऽचलः ।

tatastatenābhihatovānareṇamahātmanā ||6.59.62|| daśagrīvḥsamādhūtoyathābhūmicale'calḥ |

Thereupon, struck by the great soul, Vanara, the ten headed Ravana was shaken like the earthquake on the mountain together.

63Yuddha Kanda 59.63

सङ्ग्रामेतंतथादृष्टवारावणंतलताडितम् ।।6.59.63।। ऋषयोवानराःसिद्धानेदुर्देवास्सहासुरैः ।

saṅgrāmetṃtathādṛṣṭavārāvaṇṃtalatāḍitam ||6.59.63|| ṛṣayovānarāḥsiddhānedurdevāssahāsuraiḥ |

Ravana hit by hand in that way in a hard manner, the siddhas, demons as well and even Devatas raised their voice.

64Yuddha Kanda 59.64

अथाऽश्वास्यमहातेजारावणोवाक्यमब्रवीत् ।।6.59.64।। साधुवानर वीर्येणश्लाघनीयोऽसिमेरिपुः ।

athā'śvāsyamahātejārāvaṇovākyamabravīt ||6.59.64|| sādhuvānara vīryeṇaślāghanīyo'simeripuḥ |

And thereafter, thus spoke extremely energetic Ravana recovering from breath, "Well done vanara. You are courageous and praiseworthy, my enemy!"

65Yuddha Kanda 59.65

रावणेनैवमुक्तस्तुमारुतिर्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ।।6.59.65।। धिगस्तुममवीर्येणयस्त्वंजीवसिरावण ।

rāvaṇenaivamuktastumārutirvākyamabravīt ||6.59.65|| dhigastumamavīryeṇayastvṃjīvasirāvaṇa |

Ravana having spoken in that way, Maruti said "Oh Ravana! What a shame on my valour, you survived."

66Yuddha Kanda 59.66

सकृत्तुप्रहरेदानींदुर्भुद्धे किंविकत्थसे ।।6.59.66।। ततस्त्वांमामिकामुष्टिर्नयिष्यतियमक्षयम् ।

sakṛttupraharedānīṃdurbhuddhe kiṃvikatthase ||6.59.66|| tatastvāṃmāmikāmuṣṭirnayiṣyatiyamakṣayam |

"O evil minded one, why do you brag You do this once more I will strike you once again with my fist and send you to the abode of Yama."

67Yuddha Kanda 59.67

तोमारतिवाक्येनकोधस्तस्यतदाऽज्वलत् ।।6.59.67।। संरक्तनयनोयत्नान्मुष्टिमुद्यम्यदक्षिणम् । पातयामासवेगेनवानरोरसिवीर्यवान् ।।6.59.68।।

tomārativākyenakodhastasyatadā'jvalat ||6.59.67|| sṃraktanayanoyatnānmuṣṭimudyamyadakṣiṇam | pātayāmāsavegenavānarorasivīryavān ||6.59.68||

Consequent to Maruti speaking like that, Ravana's eyes turned red, burning with anger. The powerful Ravana lifted his right fist instantly and brought it down on his powerful enemy.

69Yuddha Kanda 59.69

हनूमान् वक्षसिव्यूढेसञ्चचालहतःपुनः । विह्वलंतुतदादृष्टवाहनूमन्तंमहाबलम् ।।6.59.69।। रथेनातिरथशशीघ्रंनीलंप्रतिसमभ्यगात् ।

hanūmān vakṣasivyūḍhesañcacālahatḥpunḥ | vihvalṃtutadādṛṣṭavāhanūmantṃmahābalam ||6.59.69|| rathenātirathaśaśīghrṃnīlṃpratisamabhyagāt |

Hit on his chest, Hanuman got hurt again and reeled. Seeing Hanuman's power, overcome with fear, the best charioteer Ravana drove quickly towards Neela.

70Yuddha Kanda 59.70

राक्षसानामधिपतिर्धशग्रीवःप्रतापवान् ।।6.59.70।। पन्नगप्रतिमैर्भीमैःपरमर्माभिभेदिभिः । शरैरादीपयामासनीलंहरिचमूपतिम् ।।6.59.71।।

rākṣasānāmadhipatirdhaśagrīvḥpratāpavān ||6.59.70|| pannagapratimairbhīmaiḥparamarmābhibhedibhiḥ | śarairādīpayāmāsanīlṃharicamūpatim ||6.59.71||

The ten headed indomitable Lord of Rakshasas with his arrows that resembled serpents, dreadful, capable of piercing into vital parts began to strike at Neela, the leader of monkeys.

72Yuddha Kanda 59.72

सशरौघसमायस्तोनीलःकपिचमूपतिः । करेणेकैनेशैलाग्रंरक्षोधिपतयेऽसृजत् ।।6.59.72।।

saśaraughasamāyastonīlḥkapicamūpatiḥ | kareṇekaineśailāgrṃrakṣodhipataye'sṛjat ||6.59.72||

Pressured by the streams of arrows, the monkey leader, Neela single handed took up a peak of a mountain and hurled at the Lord of Rakshasas.

73Yuddha Kanda 59.73

हनूमानपितेजस्वीसमाश्वस्तोमहामनाः । विप्रेक्षमाणोयुद्धेप्सुस्सरोषमिदमब्रवीत् ।।6.59.73।। नीलेनसहसंयुक्तंरावणंराक्षसेश्वरम् । अन्येनयुद्यमानस्यनयुक्तमभिधावनम् ।।6.59.74।।

hanūmānapitejasvīsamāśvastomahāmanāḥ | viprekṣamāṇoyuddhepsussaroṣamidamabravīt ||6.59.73|| nīlenasahasṃyuktṃrāvaṇṃrākṣaseśvaram | anyenayudyamānasyanayuktamabhidhāvanam ||6.59.74||

Endowed with a great mind and energetic Hanuman became alert and seeking to fight with Ravana, feeling fresh, enraged, looking at Nila said "It is not correct to attack one who is fighting with another."

75Yuddha Kanda 59.75

रावणोऽपिमहातेजास्तंच्छृङ्गंसप्तभिःशरैः । आजघानसुतीक्ष्णाग्रैस्तद्विकीर्णंपपातह ।।6.59.75।।

rāvaṇo'pimahātejāstṃcchṛṅgṃsaptabhiḥśaraiḥ | ājaghānasutīkṣṇāgraistadvikīrṇṃpapātaha ||6.59.75||

Ravana, endowed with extraordinary energy attacked that peak hurled by Neela with seven sharp arrows and it fell broken into pieces.

76Yuddha Kanda 59.76

तद्विकीर्णंगिरेश्शृङ्गंदृष्टवाहरिचमूपतिः । कालानगिरिवजज्वालकोपेनपरवीरहा ।।6.59.76।।

tadvikīrṇṃgireśśṛṅgṃdṛṣṭavāharicamūpatiḥ | kālānagirivajajvālakopenaparavīrahā ||6.59.76||

On seeing the crushed peak, Neela, the killer of stupendous foes, shone like fire at universal destruction in anger.

77Yuddha Kanda 59.77

सोऽश्वकर्णान्धान् शालांश्चूताश्चापिसुपुष्पितान् । अन्यांश्चविविधान् वृक्षान्नीलश्चिक्षेपसंयुगे ।।6.59.77।।

so'śvakarṇāndhān śālāṃścūtāścāpisupuṣpitān | anyāṃścavividhān vṛkṣānnīlaścikṣepasṃyuge ||6.59.77||

In the battle Neela hurled Aswakarana, Sala and Mango trees in good bloom and several other trees at Ravana.

78Yuddha Kanda 59.78

सतान्वृक्षान् समासाद्यप्रतिचिच्छेदरावणः । अभ्यवर्षत्सुघोरेणशरवर्षेणपावकिम् ।।6.59.78।।

satānvṛkṣān samāsādyapraticicchedarāvaṇḥ | abhyavarṣatsughoreṇaśaravarṣeṇapāvakim ||6.59.78||

Ravana broke those trees in return and showered rain of formidable arrows at Neela.

79Yuddha Kanda 59.79

अभिवृष्टश्शरौघेणमेघेनेवमहाबलः । ह्रस्वंकृत्वाततोरूपंध्वजाग्रेनिपपातह ।।6.59.79।।

abhivṛṣṭaśśaraugheṇameghenevamahābalḥ | hrasvṃkṛtvātatorūpṃdhvajāgrenipapātaha ||6.59.79||

When Neela was covered by Ravana's arrows, it was just as clouds cover mountain peaks. Neela reduced his size and landed on top of Ravana's bow.

80Yuddha Kanda 59.80

पावकात्मजमालोक्यध्वजाग्रेसमवस्थितम् । जज्वालरावणःक्रोधात्ततोनीलोननादच ।।6.59.80।।

pāvakātmajamālokyadhvajāgresamavasthitam | jajvālarāvaṇḥkrodhāttatonīlonanādaca ||6.59.80||

Neela stood on top of the post of the bow of Ravana. On seeing that Ravana was burning in anger while Neela roared.

81Yuddha Kanda 59.81

ध्वजाग्रेधनुषश्चाग्रेकिरीटाग्रेचतंहरिम् । लक्ष्मणोऽथहनूमांश्चदृष्टवारामश्चविस्मिताः ।।6.59.81।।

dhvajāgredhanuṣaścāgrekirīṭāgrecatṃharim | lakṣmaṇo'thahanūmāṃścadṛṣṭavārāmaścavismitāḥ ||6.59.81||

Then, when Ravana was unable to release the arrow from the bow on Neela, who was shifting from the top of the post to the crown. Seeing that Lakshmana, Rama and Hanuman were amazed.

82Yuddha Kanda 59.82

रावणोऽपिमहातेजाःकपिलाघवविस्मितः । अस्त्रमाहारयामासदीप्तमाग्नेयमद्भुतम् ।।6.59.82।।

rāvaṇo'pimahātejāḥkapilāghavavismitḥ | astramāhārayāmāsadīptamāgneyamadbhutam ||6.59.82||

Ravana endowed with extraordinary energy was also wonder struck at Neela's agility. Then he took up the shining shaft presided over by fire god.

83Yuddha Kanda 59.83

ततस्तेचुक्रुशुर्हृष्टालब्दलक्षाःप्लवङ्गमाः । नीललाघवसम्भ्रान्तंदृष्टवारावणमाहवे ।।6.59.83।।

tatastecukruśurhṛṣṭālabdalakṣāḥplavaṅgamāḥ | nīlalāghavasambhrāntṃdṛṣṭavārāvaṇamāhave ||6.59.83||

Thereafter, seeing Neela's feat in the war, Ravana was agitated and the Vanaras were cheerful for having achieved their wish and shouted in joy.

84Yuddha Kanda 59.84

वानराणांचनादेवसंरब्दोरावणस्तदा । सम्भ्रमाविष्टहृदयोनकिञ्चित्प्रत्यपद्यत ।।6.59.84।।

vānarāṇāṃcanādevasṃrabdorāvaṇastadā | sambhramāviṣṭahṛdayonakiñcitpratyapadyata ||6.59.84||

Provoked by the clamour of the vanaras, Ravana's heart was full of anger and did not know what to do.

85Yuddha Kanda 59.85

आग्नेयेनाधसंयुक्तंगृहीत्वारावणश्शरम् । ध्वजशीर्षस्थितंसीलमुदैक्षतनिशाचरः ।।6.59.85।।

āgneyenādhasṃyuktṃgṛhītvārāvaṇaśśaram | dhvajaśīrṣasthitṃsīlamudaikṣataniśācarḥ ||6.59.85||

And then the night ranger Ravana taking up, fitting the arrow charged with fire, looked at Neela resting on top of the bow.

86Yuddha Kanda 59.86

ततोऽब्रवीन्महातेजारावणोराक्षसेश्वरः । कपेलाघवयुक्तोऽसिमाययापरयाऽनया ।।6.59.86।।

tato'bravīnmahātejārāvaṇorākṣaseśvarḥ | kapelāghavayukto'simāyayāparayā'nayā ||6.59.86||

Ravana endowed with extraordinary energy said, "Oh monkey, you are unfair, very tricky and further deceptive."

87Yuddha Kanda 59.87

जीवितंखलुरक्षस्वयदिशक्तोऽपिवानर । तानितान्यात्मरूपाणिसृजसित्वमनेकशः ।।6.59.87।।

jīvitṃkhalurakṣasvayadiśakto'pivānara | tānitānyātmarūpāṇisṛjasitvamanekaśḥ ||6.59.87||

"Oh monkey! You are taking different forms and doing different deeds. If you have the ability, save your life."

88Yuddha Kanda 59.88

तथापित्वांमयामुक्तस्सायोकोऽस्त्रप्रयोजितः । जीवितंपरिरक्षन्तंजीविताद्भ्रंशयिष्यति ।।6.59.88।।

tathāpitvāṃmayāmuktassāyoko'straprayojitḥ | jīvitṃparirakṣantṃjīvitādbhrṃśayiṣyati ||6.59.88||

"The arrow released by me now is charged with a missile and will destroy your life. You have been protecting (changing your forms) your life."

89Yuddha Kanda 59.89

एवमुक्त्वामहाबाहूरावणोराक्षसेश्वरः । सन्धायबाणमस्त्रणचमूपतिमताडयत् ।।6.59.89।।

evamuktvāmahābāhūrāvaṇorākṣaseśvarḥ | sandhāyabāṇamastraṇacamūpatimatāḍayat ||6.59.89||

Speaking like that, Ravana, the king of Rakshasas chanting the mantras and fixing the arrow struck the commander in chief (Neela) of the Vanara army.

90Yuddha Kanda 59.90

सोऽस्त्रयुक्तेनबाणेननीलोवक्षसिताडितः । निर्धह्यमानस्सहसानिपपातमहीतले ।।6.59.90।।

so'strayuktenabāṇenanīlovakṣasitāḍitḥ | nirdhahyamānassahasānipapātamahītale ||6.59.90||

Struck by the arrow propelled by the missile (of Ravana) on the chest, Neela fell on the ground.

91Yuddha Kanda 59.91

पितृमहात्म्यसंयोगात्मनश्चापितेजसा । जानुभ्यानुपतद्भूमौनतुप्राणैर्व्ययुज्यत ।।6.59.91।।

pitṛmahātmyasṃyogātmanaścāpitejasā | jānubhyānupatadbhūmaunatuprāṇairvyayujyata ||6.59.91||

By virtue of his father's greatness and his own power, his life did not cease but he fell on the ground.

92Yuddha Kanda 59.92

विसंज्ञंवानरंदृष्टवादशग्रीवोरणोत्सुकः । रथेनाम्बुदनादेनसौमित्रिमभिदुद्रुवे ।।6.59.92।।

visṃjñṃvānarṃdṛṣṭavādaśagrīvoraṇotsukḥ | rathenāmbudanādenasaumitrimabhidudruve ||6.59.92||

Finding the vanara unconscious the ten headed Ravana who was eager to fight went towards Saumithri in the chariot making sounds that resembled the sound of rattling of clouds.

93Yuddha Kanda 59.93

आसाद्यरणमध्येतंवारयित्वास्थितोज्वलन् । धनुर्विष्फारयामासकम्पयन्निवमेदिनीम् ।।6.59.93।।

āsādyaraṇamadhyetṃvārayitvāsthitojvalan | dhanurviṣphārayāmāsakampayannivamedinīm ||6.59.93||

In the midst of the battlefield stood Ravana, intercepting with his glowing bow making loud sounds as though shaking the earth.

94Yuddha Kanda 59.94

तमाहसौमित्रिरदीनसत्त्वोविष्फारयन्तंधनुरप्रमेयम् । अभ्येहिमामद्यनिशाचरेन्द्रनवानरांस्त्वप्रतियोद्धुमर्हः ।।6.59.94।।

tamāhasaumitriradīnasattvoviṣphārayantṃdhanuraprameyam | abhyehimāmadyaniśācarendranavānarāṃstvapratiyoddhumarhḥ ||6.59.94||

Saumithri who was not depressed in spirit (by Ravana's attack) addressing Ravana who was stretching his bow said "Oh! Lord of night rangers, Be aware. You ought not to fight with vanaras."

95Yuddha Kanda 59.95

सतस्यवाक्यंप्रतिपूर्णघोषंज्याशब्दमुग्रंचनिशम्यराजा । आसाद्यसौमित्रिमवस्थितंतंकोपान्वितोवाचमुवाचरक्षः ।।6.59.95।।

satasyavākyṃpratipūrṇaghoṣṃjyāśabdamugrṃcaniśamyarājā | āsādyasaumitrimavasthitṃtṃkopānvitovācamuvācarakṣḥ ||6.59.95||

Hearing the challenging statement of the king of Rakshasas and hearing the vibrating sound, Saumithri standing close by spoke these wrathful words.

96Yuddha Kanda 59.96

दिष्ट्यासिमेराघव दृष्टिमार्गंप्राप्तोऽन्तगामीविपरीतबुद्धिः । अस्मिन् क्षणेयास्यसिमृत्युदेशंसम्पाद्यमानोममबाणजालैः ।।6.59.96।।

diṣṭyāsimerāghava dṛṣṭimārgṃprāpto'ntagāmīviparītabuddhiḥ | asmin kṣaṇeyāsyasimṛtyudeśṃsampādyamānomamabāṇajālaiḥ ||6.59.96||

"Oh Raghava! You have come under the path of my sight. You are going to meet your end. You who are perverted in mind, struck by a stream of my arrows will reach the abode of death this very moment."

97Yuddha Kanda 59.97

तमाहसौमित्रिरविस्मयानोगर्जन्तमुद्वृत्तसिताग्रदंष्ट्रम् । राजन्नगर्जन्तिमहाप्रभावाविकत्थसेपापकृतांवरिष्ठ ।।6.59.97।।

tamāhasaumitriravismayānogarjantamudvṛttasitāgradṃṣṭram | rājannagarjantimahāprabhāvāvikatthasepāpakṛtāṃvariṣṭha ||6.59.97||

Saumithri, not surprised at the screaming of Ravana with his projected teeth, said, "You are great in sinful action. People of great valour do not display such bluster. Do not shout like that."

98Yuddha Kanda 59.98

जानामिवीर्यंतवराक्षसेन्द्रबलंप्रतापंचपराक्रमंच । अवस्थितोऽहंशरचापपाणिरागच्छकिंमोघविकत्थनेन ।।6.59.98।।

jānāmivīryṃtavarākṣasendrabalṃpratāpṃcaparākramṃca | avasthito'hṃśaracāpapāṇirāgacchakiṃmoghavikatthanena ||6.59.98||

"Your valour, heroism, strength and prowess are known to me. I stand firmly with bow and arrow come on. What is the use of bragging"

99Yuddha Kanda 59.99

सएवमुक्तःकुपितस्ससर्जरक्षोथिपस्सप्तशरान् सुपुङ्खान् । तान्लक्ष्मणःकाञ्चनचित्रिपुङ्खैश्चिच्छेदबाणैर्निशिताग्रधारैः ।।6.59.99।।

saevamuktḥkupitassasarjarakṣothipassaptaśarān supuṅkhān | tānlakṣmaṇḥkāñcanacitripuṅkhaiścicchedabāṇairniśitāgradhāraiḥ ||6.59.99||

Lakshmana, having spoken in that manner, the Lord of Rakshasas released seven arrows with beautiful plumes. Lakshmana tore them into pieces with sharp pointed and sharpheaded arrows with gold shafts.

100Yuddha Kanda 59.100

तान् प्रेक्षमाणस्सहसानिकृत्तानिकृत्तभोगानिवपन्नगेन्द्रान् । लङ्केश्वरःक्रोधवशंजगामससर्जचान्यान्निशितान्पृषत्कान् ।।6.59.100।।

tān prekṣamāṇassahasānikṛttānikṛttabhogānivapannagendrān | laṅkeśvarḥkrodhavaśṃjagāmasasarjacānyānniśitānpṛṣatkān ||6.59.100||

Seeing the arrows cut down with force broken pieces like coils of serpents, the Lord of Lanka overtaken by anger let go another sharp arrow.

101Yuddha Kanda 59.101

सबाणवर्षंतुववर्षतीव्रंरामानुजःकार्मुकसम्प्रयुक्तम् । क्षुरार्धचन्द्रोत्तमकर्णिभल्लैःशरांश्चचिच्छेदनचुक्षुभेच ।।6.59.101।।

sabāṇavarṣṃtuvavarṣatīvrṃrāmānujḥkārmukasamprayuktam | kṣurārdhacandrottamakarṇibhallaiḥśarāṃścacicchedanacukṣubheca ||6.59.101||

The brother of Rama stretched his bow into a circle and showered rain of piercing arrows. He tore the arrows of his enemy with his arrows Kshura, Ardhachandra, karni and Balan without nay, perturbance.

102Yuddha Kanda 59.102

सबाणजालान्यथतानितानिमोघानिपश्यंस्त्रिदशारिराजः । विसिस्मियेलक्ष्मणलाघवेनपुनश्चबाणान्निशितान्मुमोच ।।6.59.102।।

sabāṇajālānyathatānitānimoghānipaśyṃstridaśārirājḥ | visismiyelakṣmaṇalāghavenapunaścabāṇānniśitānmumoca ||6.59.102||

Ravana, the enemy of gods seeing all his streams of arrows ineffective, was astonished at Lakshmana's skill, and let go sharp arrows again and again.

103Yuddha Kanda 59.103

सलक्ष्मणश्चापिशरान् शिताग्रान्महेन्द्रतुल्योऽशनितुल्यवेगान् । सन्धायचापेज्वलनप्रकाशान् ससर्जरक्षोधिपतेर्वधाय ।।6.59.103।।

salakṣmaṇaścāpiśarān śitāgrānmahendratulyo'śanitulyavegān | sandhāyacāpejvalanaprakāśān sasarjarakṣodhipatervadhāya ||6.59.103||

Lakshmana also, who is comparable to Mahendra, fitting his bow and arrows that shone like fire released sharp and pointed arrows to put an end to Rakshasa king.

104Yuddha Kanda 59.104

सतान् प्रचिच्छेदहिराक्षसेन्द्रःछित्वाचतन्लक्ष्मणमाजघान । शरेणकालाग्निसमप्रभेणस्वयम्भुदत्तेनललाटदेशे ।।6.59.104।।

satān pracicchedahirākṣasendrḥchitvācatanlakṣmaṇamājaghāna | śareṇakālāgnisamaprabheṇasvayambhudattenalalāṭadeśe ||6.59.104||

The Rakshasa king broke into pieces those arrows of Lakshmana and struck him on the forehead with the arrow gifted to him by Brahma, that was bright like the fire at the time of universal destruction.

105Yuddha Kanda 59.105

सलक्ष्मणोरावणसायकार्तश्चचालचापंशिधिलंप्रगृह्य । पुनश्चसंज्ञांप्रतिलभ्यकृच्छ्राच्चिच्छेदचापंत्रिदशेन्द्रशत्रो ।।6.59.105।।

salakṣmaṇorāvaṇasāyakārtaścacālacāpṃśidhilṃpragṛhya | punaścasṃjñāṃpratilabhyakṛcchrāccicchedacāpṃtridaśendraśatro ||6.59.105||

Lakshmana hurt by Ravana's dart, shaken up, seizing the bow feebly with great difficulty regaining consciousness tore the bow of the enemy of Indra into pieces in return.

106Yuddha Kanda 59.106

निकृत्तचापंत्रिभिराजघानबाणैस्तदादाशरथिश्शिताग्रैः । ससायकार्तोविचचालराजाकृच्छ्राच्छसंज्ञांपुनराससाद ।।6.59.106।।

nikṛttacāpṃtribhirājaghānabāṇaistadādāśarathiśśitāgraiḥ | sasāyakārtovicacālarājākṛcchrācchasṃjñāṃpunarāsasāda ||6.59.106||

Then Lakshmana son of Dasaratha, struck the bow of the king into pieces with three sharp and pointed arrows. The king swooned and recovered with great difficulty.

107Yuddha Kanda 59.107

सकृत्तचापःशरताडितश्चमेदार्द्रगात्रोरुधिरावसिक्तः । जग्राहशक्तिंस्वयमुग्रशक्तिस्स्वयम्भुदत्तांयुधिदेवशत्रुः ।।6.59.107।।

sakṛttacāpḥśaratāḍitaścamedārdragātrorudhirāvasiktḥ | jagrāhaśaktiṃsvayamugraśaktissvayambhudattāṃyudhidevaśatruḥ ||6.59.107||

Hurt by the arrows, bow cut, bathed in blood, limbs splattered with fat, Ravana the enemy of gods, himself being endowed with formidable energy gifted by the Self born creator seized javelin on the battlefield.

108Yuddha Kanda 59.108

सतांसधूमानलसन्निकाशांवित्रासनींसंयतिवानराणाम् । चिक्षेपशक्तिंतरसाज्वलन्तींसौमित्रयेराक्षसराष्ट्रनाथः ।।6.59.108।।

satāṃsadhūmānalasannikāśāṃvitrāsanīṃsṃyativānarāṇām | cikṣepaśaktiṃtarasājvalantīṃsaumitrayerākṣasarāṣṭranāthḥ ||6.59.108||

The Lord of the world of Rakshasas instantly flung javelin, emitting fire without smoke, on Saumithri, which created fear among Vanaras.

109Yuddha Kanda 59.109

तामापतन्तींभरतानुजोऽस्त्रैर्जघानबाणैश्चहुताग्निकल्पैः । तथापिसातस्यविवेशशक्तिर्भुजान्तरंदाशरथेर्विशालम् ।।6.59.109।।

tāmāpatantīṃbharatānujo'strairjaghānabāṇaiścahutāgnikalpaiḥ | tathāpisātasyaviveśaśaktirbhujāntarṃdāśaratherviśālam ||6.59.109||

Lakshmana, the brother of Bharata struck the fierce weapon coming towards him like flames of fire fuelled by ghee, even then it struck him on his broad chest.

110Yuddha Kanda 59.110

सशक्तिमान् शक्तिसमाहतःसन् प्रजज्वालमुहुःरघुप्रवीरः । तंविह्वलन्तंसहसाभ्युपेत्यजग्राहराजातरसाभुजाभ्याम् ।।6.59.110।।

saśaktimān śaktisamāhatḥsan prajajvālamuhuḥraghupravīrḥ | tṃvihvalantṃsahasābhyupetyajagrāharājātarasābhujābhyām ||6.59.110||

Lakshmana the eminent hero of Raghu's was badly hurt by the javelin and was burning in pain. King Ravana caught him who was losing consciousness, violently and held him by his shoulders.

111Yuddha Kanda 59.111

हिमवान् मन्दरोमेरुस्तैलोक्यंवासहामरैः । शक्यंभुजाभ्यामुद्धर्तुंनसङ् ख्येभरतानुजः ।।6.59.111।।

himavān mandaromerustailokyṃvāsahāmaraiḥ | śakyṃbhujābhyāmuddhartuṃnasaṅ khyebharatānujḥ ||6.59.111||

It may be possible to lift Himalayas, Mandara, Meru mountains or even the Devatas of the three worlds but not possible to lift Lakshmana by shoulders.

112Yuddha Kanda 59.112

शक्त्याब्राह्म्याहिसौमित्रिस्ताडितास्तुस्तनान्तरे । विष्णोरचिन्यन्त्यंस्वंभागमात्मानंप्रत्यनुस्मरत् ।।6.59.112।।

śaktyābrāhmyāhisaumitristāḍitāstustanāntare | viṣṇoracinyantyṃsvṃbhāgamātmānṃpratyanusmarat ||6.59.112||

Even though Saumithri was wounded in the chest and the power of Brahma entered (into his chest) he recalled himself as an inconceivable form of Vishnu.

113Yuddha Kanda 59.113

ततोदानवदर्पघ्नंसौमित्रिंदेवकण्टकः । तंपीडयित्वाबाहुभ्यांनप्रभुर्लङ्घनेऽभवत् ।।6.59.113।।

tatodānavadarpaghnṃsaumitriṃdevakaṇṭakḥ | tṃpīḍayitvābāhubhyāṃnaprabhurlaṅghane'bhavat ||6.59.113||

Thereafter, Ravana the enemy of Devatas was not capable of lifting Lakshmana's shoulders. Lakshmana could crush the pride of Rakshasas.

114Yuddha Kanda 59.114

अथवायुसुतःक्रुद्धोरावणंसमभिद्रवत् । आजघानोरसिक्रुद्धोवज्रकल्पेनमुष्टिना ।।6.59.114।।

athavāyusutḥkruddhorāvaṇṃsamabhidravat | ājaghānorasikruddhovajrakalpenamuṣṭinā ||6.59.114||

Then the son of the wind god, enraged, came towards Ravana. Full of anger, he struck him with his thunderbolt like fist on the chest of Ravana.

115Yuddha Kanda 59.115

तेनमुष्टिप्रहारेणरावणोराक्षसेश्वरः । जानुभ्यामपतद्भूमौचचालचपपातच ।।6.59.115।।

tenamuṣṭiprahāreṇarāvaṇorākṣaseśvarḥ | jānubhyāmapatadbhūmaucacālacapapātaca ||6.59.115||

By the blow of Hanuman's fist, Ravana the Lord of Rakshasas was shaken and fell on the ground on his knees.

116Yuddha Kanda 59.116

अस्यैश्चनेत्रैश्श्रवणैःपपातरुधिरंबहु । विघूर्णमानोनिश्चेष्टोरथोपस्थउपाविशत् ।।6.59.116।।

asyaiścanetraiśśravaṇaiḥpapātarudhirṃbahu | vighūrṇamānoniśceṣṭorathopasthaupāviśat ||6.59.116||

Much blood flowed from his(Ravana's) mouth, ears, and eyes. Rolling in the hind part of the chariot he became still and sank.

117Yuddha Kanda 59.117

विसंज्ञोमूर्च्छ्रितश्चासीन्नचस्थानंसमालभत् । विसंज्ञंरावणंदृष्टवासमरेभीमविक्रमम् ।।6.59.117।। ऋषयोवानराश्चैवनेदुर्देवास्सवासवाः ।

visṃjñomūrcchritaścāsīnnacasthānṃsamālabhat | visṃjñṃrāvaṇṃdṛṣṭavāsamarebhīmavikramam ||6.59.117|| ṛṣayovānarāścaivanedurdevāssavāsavāḥ |

Ravana of fierce valour having lost senses and waking up from unconscious state, perturbed, did not know where he was. Seeing this the sages, even Vanaras, the demons as well as Devatas shouted in joy.

118Yuddha Kanda 59.118

हनूमानपितेजस्वीलक्ष्मणंरावणार्धितम् ।।6.59.118।। आनयद्राघवाभ्याशंबाहुभ्यांपरिगृह्यतम् ।

hanūmānapitejasvīlakṣmaṇṃrāvaṇārdhitam ||6.59.118|| ānayadrāghavābhyāśṃbāhubhyāṃparigṛhyatam |

Brilliant Hanuman holding Lakshmana wounded by Ravana in both shoulders brought him to Rama.

119Yuddha Kanda 59.119

वायुसूनोःसुहृत्त्वेनभक्त्यापरमयाचसः ।।6.59.119।। शत्रूणामप्रकम्प्योऽपिलघुत्वमगमत्कपेः ।

vāyusūnoḥsuhṛttvenabhaktyāparamayācasḥ ||6.59.119|| śatrūṇāmaprakampyo'pilaghutvamagamatkapeḥ |

Lakshmana though unshakeable for enemies, became light for the son of wind god by his friendship and high devotion.

120Yuddha Kanda 59.120

तंसमुत्सृज्यसाशक्तिःसौमित्रिंयुधिदुर्जयम् ।।6.59.120।। रावणस्यरथेतस्मिन् स्थानंपुनरुपागता ।

tṃsamutsṛjyasāśaktiḥsaumitriṃyudhidurjayam ||6.59.120|| rāvaṇasyarathetasmin sthānṃpunarupāgatā |

That power returned back to Ravana's chariot to its position, leaving Lakshmana, who was difficult to vanquish.

121Yuddha Kanda 59.121

आश्वस्तश्चनिशल्यश्चलक्ष्मणश्शत्रुसूदनः ।।6.59.121।। विष्णोर्भागममीमांस्यमात्मानंप्रत्यनुस्मरन् ।

āśvastaścaniśalyaścalakṣmaṇaśśatrusūdanḥ ||6.59.121|| viṣṇorbhāgamamīmāṃsyamātmānṃpratyanusmaran |

Lakshmana who could destroy foes, recovering from unconsciousness, relieved of wounds again remembered himself to be part of Vishnu of immeasurable power.

122Yuddha Kanda 59.122

रावणोऽपिमहातेजाःप्राप्यसंज्ञांमहाहवे ।।6.59.122।। आददेनिशितान् बाणान् जग्राहचमहद्धमः ।

rāvaṇo'pimahātejāḥprāpyasṃjñāṃmahāhave ||6.59.122|| ādadeniśitān bāṇān jagrāhacamahaddhamḥ |

Ravana of mighty prowess waking up from consciousness took over his sharp arrows and the great bow also.

123Yuddha Kanda 59.123

निपातितमहावीरांद्रवन्तींवानरींचमूम् ।।6.59.123।। राघवस्तुरणेदृष्टवारावणंसमभिद्रवत् ।

nipātitamahāvīrāṃdravantīṃvānarīṃcamūm ||6.59.123|| rāghavasturaṇedṛṣṭavārāvaṇṃsamabhidravat |

Raghava also assailed Ravana seeing the fallen Vanara heroes struck in the combat.

124Yuddha Kanda 59.124

अथैवमुपसङ्गम्यहनूमान्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ।।6.59.124।। ममपृष्ठंसमारुह्यराक्षसंशास्तुमर्हसि । विष्णुर्यथागरुत्मन्तंबलवन्तंसमाहितः ।।6.59.125।।

athaivamupasaṅgamyahanūmānvākyamabravīt ||6.59.124|| mamapṛṣṭhṃsamāruhyarākṣasṃśāstumarhasi | viṣṇuryathāgarutmantṃbalavantṃsamāhitḥ ||6.59.125||

And then Hanuman submitted to Rama, "Just as Lord Vishnu mounted on mighty Garuda, you should also be mounted on my back and subdue Ravana."

126Yuddha Kanda 59.126

तच्छ्रुत्वाराघवोवाक्यंवायुपुत्रेणभाषितम् । आरुरोहमहाशूरंबलवन्तंमहाकपिम् ।।6.59.126।। रथस्थंरावणंसङ् ख्येददर्शमनुजाधिपः ।

tacchrutvārāghavovākyṃvāyuputreṇabhāṣitam | ārurohamahāśūrṃbalavantṃmahākapim ||6.59.126|| rathasthṃrāvaṇṃsaṅ khyedadarśamanujādhipḥ |

On hearing the sub mission of Hanuman, Sri Rama, the Lord of humans climbed on the great monkey and saw Ravana on the chariot.

127Yuddha Kanda 59.127

तमालोक्यमहातेजाःप्रदुद्रावसराघवः ।।6.59.127।। वैरोचनिवक्रुद्धोविष्णुरभ्युद्यतायुधः ।

tamālokyamahātejāḥpradudrāvasarāghavḥ ||6.59.127|| vairocanivakruddhoviṣṇurabhyudyatāyudhḥ |

Beholding Ravana, highly energetic Rama wielding his bow went furiously towards him just as Lord Vishnu went with his discuss against Virochana.

128Yuddha Kanda 59.128

ज्याशब्दमकरोत्तीव्रंवज्रनिष्पेषनिस्वनम् ।।6.59.128।। गिरागम्भीरयारामोराक्षसेन्द्रमुवाचह ।

jyāśabdamakarottīvrṃvajraniṣpeṣanisvanam ||6.59.128|| girāgambhīrayārāmorākṣasendramuvācaha |

Rama created a sound like a clap of thunder ringing intensely, a majestic one and spoke in a deep voice to the king.

129Yuddha Kanda 59.129

तिष्ठतिष्ठममत्वंहिकृत्वाविप्रियमीदृशम् ।।6.59.129।। क्वनुराक्षसशार्दूल गतोमोक्षमवाप्स्यसि ।

tiṣṭhatiṣṭhamamatvṃhikṛtvāvipriyamīdṛśam ||6.59.129|| kvanurākṣasaśārdūla gatomokṣamavāpsyasi |

"Oh! Tiger among Rakshasas, forbear, forbear. Having done such an offence to me, where are you going for deliverance"

130Yuddha Kanda 59.130

यदीन्द्रवैवस्वतभास्करान्वास्वयम्भुवैश्वानरशंकरान्वा । गमिष्यसित्वंदशवादिशोवातथापिमेनाद्यगतोविमोक्ष्यसे ।।6.59.130।।

yadīndravaivasvatabhāskarānvāsvayambhuvaiśvānaraśṃkarānvā | gamiṣyasitvṃdaśavādiśovātathāpimenādyagatovimokṣyase ||6.59.130||

"If you go to Indra or Yama or Sun God or the selfborn Brahma or fire God or Shankara or the ten directions like that, and if you run in front of me you cannot escape."

131Yuddha Kanda 59.131

यश्चैवशक्त्याभिहतस्त्वयाऽद्यइच्छन्विषादंसहसाभ्युपेत्य । सएषरक्षोगणराजमृत्युःसपुत्रपौत्रस्यतवाद्ययुद्धे ।।6.59.131।।

yaścaivaśaktyābhihatastvayā'dyaicchanviṣādṃsahasābhyupetya | saeṣarakṣogaṇarājamṛtyuḥsaputrapautrasyatavādyayuddhe ||6.59.131||

"Oh! king of Rakshasa troop! He, who was struck without support now, even if he were to come now in front, you with your sons and grandsons will be struck to death by him."

132Yuddha Kanda 59.132

एतेनचात्यद्भुतदर्शनानिशरैर्जवस्थानकृतालयानि । चतुर्धशान्यात्तवरायुधानिरक्षःसहस्राणिनिषूदितानि ।।6.59.132।।

etenacātyadbhutadarśanāniśarairjavasthānakṛtālayāni | caturdhaśānyāttavarāyudhānirakṣḥsahasrāṇiniṣūditāni ||6.59.132||

"By me, who is your adversary, fourteen thousand Rakshasas of fierce forms, who had taken abode at Janasthana and used choicest weapons were exterminated."

133Yuddha Kanda 59.133

राघवस्यवचश्श्रुत्वाराक्षसेन्द्रोमहाबलः । वायुपुत्रंमहावेगंवहन्तंराघवंरणे ।।6.59.133।। रोषेणमहताविष्टःपूर्ववैरमनुस्मरन् । आजघानशरैर्दीप्स्सैःकालानलशिखोपमैः ।।6.59.134।।

rāghavasyavacaśśrutvārākṣasendromahābalḥ | vāyuputrṃmahāvegṃvahantṃrāghavṃraṇe ||6.59.133|| roṣeṇamahatāviṣṭḥpūrvavairamanusmaran | ājaghānaśarairdīpssaiḥkālānalaśikhopamaiḥ ||6.59.134||

On hearing Raghava's words, the mighty strong king of Rakshasas seized with violent anger, recalling earlier enmity, struck with blazing arrows like tongues of fire at the time of universal destruction while Hanuman bore Rama.

135Yuddha Kanda 59.135

राक्षसेनाहवेतस्यताडितस्यापिसायकैः । स्वभावतेजोयुक्तस्यभूयस्तेजोऽभ्यवर्धत ।।6.59.135।।

rākṣasenāhavetasyatāḍitasyāpisāyakaiḥ | svabhāvatejoyuktasyabhūyastejo'bhyavardhata ||6.59.135||

Even though Hanuman was hurt by the arrows discharged by the Rakshasa king, being endowed with energy by nature, his energy went on increasing.

136Yuddha Kanda 59.136

ततोरामोमहातेजारावणेनकृतव्रणम् । दृष्टवाप्लवगशार्दूलंक्रोधस्यवशमेयिवान् ।।6.59.136।।

tatorāmomahātejārāvaṇenakṛtavraṇam | dṛṣṭavāplavagaśārdūlṃkrodhasyavaśameyivān ||6.59.136||

Seeing the wounds caused by Ravana to Hanuman, the tiger among monkeys, possibly Rama was seized with anger.

137Yuddha Kanda 59.137

तस्याभिसङ्क्रम्यरथंसचक्रंसाश्वध्वजच्छत्रमहापताकम् । ससारथिंसाशनिशूलखडगंरामःप्रचिच्छेदशरैस्सुपुङ्खैः ।।6.59.137।।

tasyābhisaṅkramyarathṃsacakrṃsāśvadhvajacchatramahāpatākam | sasārathiṃsāśaniśūlakhaḍagṃrāmḥpracicchedaśaraissupuṅkhaiḥ ||6.59.137||

Advancing towards him, (Ravana), Rama tore into pieces his chariot, wheels, horses, standard and canopy including the charioteer and the darts and swords.

138Yuddha Kanda 59.138

अथेन्द्रशत्रुंतरसाजघानबाणेनवज्राशनिसन्निभेन । भुजान्तरेव्यूढसुजातरूपेवज्रेणमेरुंभगवानिवेन्द्रः ।।6.59.138।।

athendraśatruṃtarasājaghānabāṇenavajrāśanisannibhena | bhujāntarevyūḍhasujātarūpevajreṇameruṃbhagavānivendrḥ ||6.59.138||

Then with a shining thunderbolt like arrows Rama struck with the weapon of Indra into the fine chest in between the shoulders just as Indra would strike the Meru Mountain with his thunderbolt.

139Yuddha Kanda 59.139

योवज्रपातशनिसन्निपातान्नचक्षुभेनापिचचालराजा । सरामबाणाभिहतोभृशार्तश्चचालचापंचमुमोचवीरः ।।6.59.139।।

yovajrapātaśanisannipātānnacakṣubhenāpicacālarājā | sarāmabāṇābhihatobhṛśārtaścacālacāpṃcamumocavīrḥ ||6.59.139||

He, that heroic king, who was not disturbed hurt by thunderbolt of lightning, was agonized when struck by Rama's arrow and let his bow fall.

140Yuddha Kanda 59.140

तंविह्वलन्तंप्रसमीक्ष्यरामस्समाददेदीप्तमथार्थचन्द्रम् । तेनार्कवर्णंसहसाकिरीटंचिच्छेदरक्षोधिपतेर्महात्मा ।।6.59.140।।

tṃvihvalantṃprasamīkṣyarāmassamādadedīptamathārthacandram | tenārkavarṇṃsahasākirīṭṃcicchedarakṣodhipatermahātmā ||6.59.140||

Seeing Ravana swooning, great soul Rama took up a crescent shaped arrow and hastily broke his crown into pieces which were shining bright red like the Sun.

141Yuddha Kanda 59.141

तंनिर्विषाशीविषसन्निकाशंशान्तार्चिषंसूर्यमिवाप्रकाशम् । गतश्रियंकृत्तकिरीटकूटमुवाचरामोयुधिराक्षसेन्द्रम् ।।6.59.141।।

tṃnirviṣāśīviṣasannikāśṃśāntārciṣṃsūryamivāprakāśam | gataśriyṃkṛttakirīṭakūṭamuvācarāmoyudhirākṣasendram ||6.59.141||

Rama said to the king of Rakshasas, who was like a serpent rid of poison without (head) crown, without splendour like the Sun without brightness, his glory lost with the crown broken in the combat.

142Yuddha Kanda 59.142

कृतंत्वयाकर्ममहत्सुभीमंहतप्रवीरश्चकृतस्त्वयाऽहम् । तस्मात्परिश्रान्तइतिव्यवस्यन्नत्वांशरैर्मृत्युवशंनयामि ।।6.59.142।।

kṛtṃtvayākarmamahatsubhīmṃhatapravīraścakṛtastvayā'ham | tasmātpariśrāntaitivyavasyannatvāṃśarairmṛtyuvaśṃnayāmi ||6.59.142||

A great task of killing mighty heroes has been accomplished by you. Therefore, you are exhausted. I declare that I will not send you to death.

143Yuddha Kanda 59.143

गच्छानुजानामिरणार्धितस्त्वंप्रविश्यरात्रिंचरराजलङ्काम् । आश्वस्यनिर्याहिरथीचधन्वीतदाबलंप्रेक्ष्यसिमेरथस्थः ।।6.59.143।।

gacchānujānāmiraṇārdhitastvṃpraviśyarātriṃcararājalaṅkām | āśvasyaniryāhirathīcadhanvītadābalṃprekṣyasimerathasthḥ ||6.59.143||

"King of night rangers! I know you are agonized after the war. Go to Lanka and rest and reenter, mounted on your chariot with bow. You will witness my strength."

144Yuddha Kanda 59.144

सएवमुक्तोहतदर्पहर्षोनिकृत्तचापश्चहताश्वसूतः । शरार्दितःकृत्तमहाकिरीटोविवेशलङ्कांसहसास्मराजा ।।6.59.144।।

saevamuktohatadarpaharṣonikṛttacāpaścahatāśvasūtḥ | śarārditḥkṛttamahākirīṭoviveśalaṅkāṃsahasāsmarājā ||6.59.144||

Rama having spoken in that way (consoling), the king with his vanity and pride crushed, bow broken into pieces, horses and charioteer killed, wounded by arrows, crown broken entered Lanka hastily.

145Yuddha Kanda 59.145

प्रविष्टेरजनीचरेन्द्रेमहाबलेदाववदेवशत्रौ । हरीन् विशल्यान् सहलक्ष्मणेनचकाररामःपरमाहवाग्रे ।।6.59.145।।

praviṣṭerajanīcarendremahābaledāvavadevaśatrau | harīn viśalyān sahalakṣmaṇenacakārarāmḥparamāhavāgre ||6.59.145||

After the king of prowlers, the enemy of Danavas and Devas entered Lanka, Rama and Lakshmana also drew out the arrows from monkeys in the forefront of the battlefield and went.

146Yuddha Kanda 59.146

तस्मिन्प्रभन्नेत्रिदशेन्द्रशतौसुरासुराभूतगणादिशश्च । ससागरास्सर्षिमहोरगाश्चतथैवभूम्यम्बुचराश्चप्रहृष्टाः ।।6.59.146।।

tasminprabhannetridaśendraśatausurāsurābhūtagaṇādiśaśca | sasāgarāssarṣimahoragāścatathaivabhūmyambucarāścaprahṛṣṭāḥ ||6.59.146||

The enemy of Indra having been routed, the suras and asuras, the guardians of the four quarters, including the sea, the large serpents, and sages so also the creatures of land and water were joyful. ।।इत्यार्षेवाल्मीकीयेश्रीमद्रामायणेआदिकाव्येयुद्धकाण्डेएकोनषष्टितमस्सर्गः ।। This is the end of the fifty ninth sarga of Yuddha Kanda of the first epic the holy Ramayana composed by sage Valmiki.
