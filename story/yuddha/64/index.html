Yuddha Kanda•Sarga 64

Sarga 64

Valmiki Ramayana · Yuddha Kanda · 34 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 64 / 131
1Yuddha Kanda 64.1

तदुक्तमतिकायस्यबलिनोबाहुशालिनः । कुम्भकर्णस्यवचनंश्रुत्वोवाचमहोदरः ।।6.64.1।।

taduktamatikāyasyabalinobāhuśālinḥ | kumbhakarṇasyavacanṃśrutvovācamahodarḥ ||6.64.1||

Hearing the words of mighty Kumbhakarna, of massive body and round arms Mahodara said.

2Yuddha Kanda 64.2

कुम्भकर्णकुलेजातोधृष्टःप्राकृतदर्शनः । अवलिप्तो न शक्नोषिकृत्यंसर्वत्रवेदितुम् ।।6.64.2।।

kumbhakarṇakulejātodhṛṣṭḥprākṛtadarśanḥ | avalipto na śaknoṣikṛtyṃsarvatraveditum ||6.64.2||

"Kumbhakarna! Although you are born of noble race, you are insolent, seeing your low intellect in all aspects you have no knowledge of what should be done or not to be done."

4Yuddha Kanda 64.4

स्थानंवृद्धिं च हानि च देशकालविभागवित् । आत्मनश्चपरेषां च बुध्यतेराक्षसर्षभः ।।6.64.4।।

sthānṃvṛddhiṃ ca hāni ca deśakālavibhāgavit | ātmanaścapareṣāṃ ca budhyaterākṣasarṣabhḥ ||6.64.4||

"Ravana, a bull among Rakshasas is conversant with rules of time and place and knows his own enemies, his progress and decline also."

5Yuddha Kanda 64.5

यत्तुशक्यंबलवताकर्तुंप्राकृतबुद्धिना । अनुपासितवृद्धेनकःकुर्यात्तादृशंनरः ।।6.64.5।।

yattuśakyṃbalavatākartuṃprākṛtabuddhinā | anupāsitavṛddhenakḥkuryāttādṛśṃnarḥ ||6.64.5||

" A strong person of unsophisticated intellect, who is progressive, a knower of everything will do such a task which is not possible to do?"

6Yuddha Kanda 64.6

यांस्तुधर्मार्थकामांस्त्वंब्रवीषिपृथगाश्रयान् । अवबोद्धुंस्वभावेतान्नहिलक्षणमस्तितान् ।।6.64.6।।

yāṃstudharmārthakāmāṃstvṃbravīṣipṛthagāśrayān | avaboddhuṃsvabhāvetānnahilakṣaṇamastitān ||6.64.6||

"The virtues, worldly gain and sensuous pleasures give results separately as you said. They are incompatible with what you said and that it is its nature you said. what is the proof?"

7Yuddha Kanda 64.7

कर्मचैवहिसर्वेषांकारणानांप्रयोजकम् । श्रेयःपापीयसांचात्रफलंभवतिकर्मणाम् ।।6.64.7।।

karmacaivahisarveṣāṃkāraṇānāṃprayojakam | śreyḥpāpīyasāṃcātraphalṃbhavatikarmaṇām ||6.64.7||

" Actions alone are the means of happiness. Being so how can sinful actions accrue good results?"

8Yuddha Kanda 64.8

निश्श्रेयसफलावेवधर्मार्थावितरावपि । अधर्मानर्थयोःप्राप्तिःफलं च प्रत्यवायिकम् ।।6.64.8।।

niśśreyasaphalāvevadharmārthāvitarāvapi | adharmānarthayoḥprāptiḥphalṃ ca pratyavāyikam ||6.64.8||

" All virtues and worldly pleasures and even otherwise give only benefit. But if done with disinterest and unrighteousness it can give a result which is other than prosperity."

9Yuddha Kanda 64.9

ऐहलौकिकपारक्यंकर्मपुम्भिर्निषेव्यते । कर्माण्यपितुकल्यानिलभतेकाममास्थितः ।।6.64.9।।

aihalaukikapārakyṃkarmapumbhirniṣevyate | karmāṇyapitukalyānilabhatekāmamāsthitḥ ||6.64.9||

" All beings reap the fruit of their actions in this world as well as in other worlds. He, who remains to be pleasure seeking, devoted to actions will receive blessings and attain pleasures in this life only (not here after)."

10Yuddha Kanda 64.10

तत्रक्लुप्तमिदंराज्ञाहृदिकार्यंमतं च नः । शत्रौहिसाहसंयत्स्यात्किमिवात्रापनीयते ।।6.64.10।।

tatrakluptamidṃrājñāhṛdikāryṃmatṃ ca nḥ | śatrauhisāhasṃyatsyātkimivātrāpanīyate ||6.64.10||

" At that time the king intended in his heart (to abduct Sita), and we approved his action, and it was done. What is wrong in doing such a courageous action against an enemy?"

11Yuddha Kanda 64.11

एकस्यैवाभियानेतुहेतुर्यःकथितस्त्वया । तत्राप्यनुपपन्नंतेवक्ष्यामियदसाधु च ।।6.64.11।।

ekasyaivābhiyānetuheturyḥkathitastvayā | tatrāpyanupapannṃtevakṣyāmiyadasādhu ca ||6.64.11||

"You have set out alone (for war) and the reason adduced by you is ill conceived and improper. I shall tell you."

12Yuddha Kanda 64.12

येनपूर्वंजनस्थानेबहवोऽतिबलाहताः । राक्षसाराघवंतंत्वंकथमेकोजयिष्यसि ।।6.64.12।।

yenapūrvṃjanasthānebahavo'tibalāhatāḥ | rākṣasārāghavṃtṃtvṃkathamekojayiṣyasi ||6.64.12||

"How is it possible for you single handed to win Raghava of exceeding might, who killed many Rakshasas in the past at Janasthana?"

13Yuddha Kanda 64.13

येपुरानिर्जितास्तेनजनस्थानेमहौजसः । राक्षसांस्तान्पुरेसर्वान्भीतानद्यापिपश्यसि ।।6.64.13।।

yepurānirjitāstenajanasthānemahaujasḥ | rākṣasāṃstānpuresarvānbhītānadyāpipaśyasi ||6.64.13||

"Look! Those highly energetic Rakshasas, who were won by Rama at Janasthana are scared of him even now."

14Yuddha Kanda 64.14

तंसिंहमिवसङ्क्रुद्धंरामंदशरथात्मजम् । सर्पंसुप्तमिवबुद्ध्यप्रबोधयितुमिच्छसि ।।6.64.14।।

tṃsiṃhamivasaṅkruddhṃrāmṃdaśarathātmajam | sarpṃsuptamivabuddhyaprabodhayitumicchasi ||6.64.14||

"Do you desire to awaken Rama, who is like an enraged lion knowingly, who is like a sleeping snake?"

15Yuddha Kanda 64.15

ज्वलन्तंतेजसानित्यंक्रोधेन च दुरासदम् । कस्तंमृत्युमिवासह्यमासादयितुमर्हति ।।6.64.15।।

jvalantṃtejasānityṃkrodhena ca durāsadam | kastṃmṛtyumivāsahyamāsādayitumarhati ||6.64.15||

"Who can dare to approach Rama, who is difficult to encounter, unbearable and burning in anger like the spirit of death."

16Yuddha Kanda 64.16

संशयस्थमिदंसर्वंशत्रोःप्रतिसमासने । एकस्यगमनंतत्र न हिमेरोचतेभृशम् ।।6.64.16।।

sṃśayasthamidṃsarvṃśatroḥpratisamāsane | ekasyagamanṃtatra na himerocatebhṛśam ||6.64.16||

"Face to face with the enemy this total army will be in danger. You're going alone is certainly not desirable."

17Yuddha Kanda 64.17

हीनार्थस्तुसमृद्धार्थंकोरिपुंप्राकृतंयथा । निश्चित्यजीवितत्यागेवशमानेतुमिच्छति ।।6.64.17।।

hīnārthastusamṛddhārthṃkoripuṃprākṛtṃyathā | niścityajīvitatyāgevaśamānetumicchati ||6.64.17||

"How can a weak person desire to go to the enemy who is rich in resources, determined to sacrifice his life several times and submit himself?"

18Yuddha Kanda 64.18

यस्यनास्तिमनुष्येषुसदृशोराक्षसोत्तम । कथमाशंससेयोद्धुंतुल्येनेन्द्रविवस्वतोः ।।6.64.18।।

yasyanāstimanuṣyeṣusadṛśorākṣasottama | kathamāśṃsaseyoddhuṃtulyenendravivasvatoḥ ||6.64.18||

"O Best of Rakshasas! How would you enter into combat with one who is great among all human beings and while even Indra and Sun God are not equal to him in war?"

19Yuddha Kanda 64.19

एवमुक्त्वातुसम्रब्दःकुम्भकर्णंमहोदरः । उवाचरक्षसांमध्येरावणंलोकरावणम् ।।6.64.19।।

evamuktvātusamrabdḥkumbhakarṇṃmahodarḥ | uvācarakṣasāṃmadhyerāvaṇṃlokarāvaṇam ||6.64.19||

Mahodara having spoken to agitated Kumbhakarna in the midst of Rakshasas in this way, submitted to Ravana, who makes people cry.

20Yuddha Kanda 64.20

लब्ध्वापुनस्त्वंवैदेहींकिमर्थंसम्प्रजल्पसि । यदीच्छसितदासीतावशगातेभविष्यति ।।6.64.20।।

labdhvāpunastvṃvaidehīṃkimarthṃsamprajalpasi | yadīcchasitadāsītāvaśagātebhaviṣyati ||6.64.20||

"Having got Vaidehi, why are you talking like that? Sita will submit to you if you desire."

21Yuddha Kanda 64.21

दृष्टःकश्चदुपायोमेसीतोपस्थानकारकः । रुचितश्चेत्स्वयाबुध्याराक्षसेन्द्र तंशृणु ।।6.64.21।।

dṛṣṭḥkaścadupāyomesītopasthānakārakḥ | rucitaścetsvayābudhyārākṣasendra tṃśṛṇu ||6.64.21||

"O King of Rakshasas! A means of bringing her round is known to me. Listen, think over with your intellect."

22Yuddha Kanda 64.22

अहंद्विजिह्वस्सम्ह्रादीकुम्भकर्णोवितर्दनः । पञ्चरामवधायैतेनिर्यान्त्वित्यवघोषय ।।6.64.22।।

ahṃdvijihvassamhrādīkumbhakarṇovitardanḥ | pañcarāmavadhāyaiteniryāntvityavaghoṣaya ||6.64.22||

"You pronounce that I, Dwijihva, Samhradri, Kumbhakarna and Vitardana, five of us will go forth to kill Rama."

23Yuddha Kanda 64.23

ततोगत्वावयंयुद्धंदास्यामस्तस्ययत्नतः । जेष्यामोयदितेशत्रून्नोपायैःकृत्यमस्तिनः ।।6.64.23।।

tatogatvāvayṃyuddhṃdāsyāmastasyayatnatḥ | jeṣyāmoyaditeśatrūnnopāyaiḥkṛtyamastinḥ ||6.64.23||

"Thereafter, proceeding forth we shall try to win your enemy. If we win, there will be no purpose for you to do any action."

24Yuddha Kanda 64.24

अथजीवतिनश्शत्रुर्वयं च कृतसम्युगाः । ततस्तदभिपत्स्यामोमनसायत्समीक्षितम् ।।6.64.24।।

athajīvatinaśśatrurvayṃ ca kṛtasamyugāḥ | tatastadabhipatsyāmomanasāyatsamīkṣitam ||6.64.24||

"If our enemy survives in combat, we too will survive and review as we planned and see."

25Yuddha Kanda 64.25

वयंयुद्धादिहेष्यामोरुधिरेणसमुक्षिताः । विदार्यस्वतनुंबाणैरामनामाङ्कितैश्शितैः ।।6.64.25।।

vayṃyuddhādiheṣyāmorudhireṇasamukṣitāḥ | vidāryasvatanuṃbāṇairāmanāmāṅkitaiśśitaiḥ ||6.64.25||

"Bathed in blood, wounded by the arrows marked with Rama's name, bodies torn cruelly we will come here from war."

26Yuddha Kanda 64.26

भक्षितोराघवोऽस्माभिर्लक्ष्मणश्चेतिवादिनः । तवपादौग्रहीष्यामस्त्वंनःकामंप्रपूरय ।।6.64.26।।

bhakṣitorāghavo'smābhirlakṣmaṇaścetivādinḥ | tavapādaugrahīṣyāmastvṃnḥkāmṃprapūraya ||6.64.26||

"We too will hold your feet saying 'Rama and Lakshmana have been devoured by us'. Then you will grant our desires."

27Yuddha Kanda 64.27

ततोऽवघोषयपुरेगजस्कन्धेनपार्थिव । हतोरामस्सहभ्रात्राससैन्यइतिसर्वतः ।।6.64.27।।

tato'vaghoṣayapuregajaskandhenapārthiva | hatorāmassahabhrātrāsasainyaitisarvatḥ ||6.64.27||

"Thereafter mounted on elephant backs all over the city will make a rakshasa pronounce that Rama accompanied by his brother are killed."

28Yuddha Kanda 64.28

प्रीतोनामततोभूत्वाभृत्यानांत्वमरिन्दम । भोगांश्चपरिवारांश्चकामांश्चवसुदापय ।।6.64.28।।

prītonāmatatobhūtvābhṛtyānāṃtvamarindama | bhogāṃścaparivārāṃścakāmāṃścavasudāpaya ||6.64.28||

"O tamer of enemies! In the name of expression of happiness, thereafter gift items of enjoyment to the attendants, and companions and granting wealth as per their desire.

29Yuddha Kanda 64.29

ततोमाल्यानिवासांसिवीराणामनुलेपनम् । पेयं च बहुयोधेभ्यस्स्वयं च मुदितःपिब ।।6.64.29।।

tatomālyānivāsāṃsivīrāṇāmanulepanam | peyṃ ca bahuyodhebhyassvayṃ ca muditḥpiba ||6.64.29||

"Thereafter give your warriors, garlands, cosmetics and many drinks and drink yourself happily."

30Yuddha Kanda 64.30

ततोऽस्मिन् बहुलीभूतेकौलीनेसर्वतोगते । भक्षितस्ससुहृद्रामोराक्षसैरितिविश्रुते ।।6.64.30।। प्रविश्याश्वास्यचापित्वंसीतांरहसिसान्त्वय । धनधान्यैश्चकामैश्चरत्नैश्चानांप्रलोभय ।।6.64.31।।

tato'smin bahulībhūtekaulīnesarvatogate | bhakṣitassasuhṛdrāmorākṣasairitiviśrute ||6.64.30|| praviśyāśvāsyacāpitvṃsītāṃrahasisāntvaya | dhanadhānyaiścakāmaiścaratnaiścānāṃpralobhaya ||6.64.31||

Thereafter, when the scandal that Rama and Lakshmana with all his friends have been devoured is widely spread everywhere, approaching Sita privately, restoring confidence and consoling her, tempt her with offering wealth, grain and gems and conciliate.

32Yuddha Kanda 64.32

अनयोपधयाराजन् भयशोकानुबन्धया । अकामात्वद्वशंसीतानष्टनाथाभविष्यति ।।6.64.32।।

anayopadhayārājan bhayaśokānubandhayā | akāmātvadvaśṃsītānaṣṭanāthābhaviṣyati ||6.64.32||

"O King! By conjuring her like this, helpless Sita overtaken by fear and sorrow having lost her husband will submit to you in spite of no desire to you."

33Yuddha Kanda 64.33

ञ्जनीयम्हिभर्तारंविनष्टमवगम्यसा । नैराश्यात् स्त्रीलघुत्वाच्चत्वद्वशंप्रतिपत्स्यते ।।6.64.33।।

ñjanīyamhibhartārṃvinaṣṭamavagamyasā | nairāśyāt strīlaghutvāccatvadvaśṃpratipatsyate ||6.64.33||

"Her charming husband ceased to live and thinking of no alternate, being a fickle woman, will accept your dependence."

34Yuddha Kanda 64.34

सापुरासुखसम्वृद्धासुखार्हादुःखकर्शिता । त्वय्यधीनंसुखंज्ञात्वासर्वथोगमिष्यति ।।6.64.34।।

sāpurāsukhasamvṛddhāsukhārhāduḥkhakarśitā | tvayyadhīnṃsukhṃjñātvāsarvathogamiṣyati ||6.64.34||

"That Sita having grown up in much comfort, deserved felicity. Tormented with grief, desperate, depending on you alone for happiness, will submit to you completely."

35Yuddha Kanda 64.35

एतत्सुनीतंममदर्शनेनरामंहिदृष्टवैभवेदनर्धः । इहैवतेसेत्स्यतिमोत्सुकोभूर्महानयुद्धेनसुखस्यलाभः ।।6.64.35।।

etatsunītṃmamadarśanenarāmṃhidṛṣṭavaibhavedanardhḥ | ihaivatesetsyatimotsukobhūrmahānayuddhenasukhasyalābhḥ ||6.64.35||

"To me this is the best course. As soon as you see Rama, do not be eager for war. It is meaningless. Great gain will accrue to you from here."

36Yuddha Kanda 64.36

अदृष्टसैन्योह्यनवाप्तसंशयोरिपूनयुद्धेनजयन् जनाधिप । यशश्चपुण्यं च महन्महीपतेश्रशियं च कीर्तिं च चिरं ।।6.64.36।।

adṛṣṭasainyohyanavāptasṃśayoripūnayuddhenajayan janādhipa | yaśaścapuṇyṃ ca mahanmahīpateśraśiyṃ ca kīrtiṃ ca cirṃ ||6.64.36||

"O Lord of the people! If a king conquers his enemy without any loss of army, without facing any dangerand without war, such a mighty ruler of the earth will indeed earn fame and merit and attain prosperity." ।।इत्यार्षेवाल्मीकीयेश्रीमद्रामायणेआदिकाव्येयुद्धकाण्डेचतुष्षष्टितमस्सर्गः।। This is the end of the sixty fourth sarga of Yuddha Kanda of the first epic the holy Ramayana composed by sage Valmiki.
