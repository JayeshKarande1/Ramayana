Sarga 6

Valmiki Ramayana · Aranya Kanda · 21 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 6 / 75
1Aranya Kanda 6.1

शरभङ्गे दिवं प्राप्ते मुनिसङ्घास्समागताः। अभ्यगच्छन्त काकुत्स्थं रामं ज्वलिततेजसम्।।3.6.1।।

śarabhaṅge divṃ prāpte munisaṅghāssamāgatāḥ| abhyagacchanta kākutsthṃ rāmṃ jvalitatejasam||3.6.1||

When Sarabhanga attained the heaven, all the sages collected together and came to Rama whose radiance was like fire.

2Aranya Kanda 6.2

वैखानसा वालखिल्यास्सम्प्रक्षाला मरीचिपाः। अश्मकुट्टाश्च बहवः पत्राहाराश्च तापसाः।।3.6.2।। दन्तोलूखलिनश्चैव तथैवोन्मज्जकाः परे। गात्रशय्या अशय्याश्च तथैवाभ्रावकाशकाः।।3.6.3।। मुनयस्सलिलाहारावायुभक्षा स्तथापरे। आकाशनिलयाश्चैव तथा स्थण्डिलशायिनः।।3.6.4।। व्रतोपवासिनो दान्तास्तथाऽर्द्रपटवाससः। सजपाश्च तपोनित्यास्तथा पञ्चतपोऽन्विताः।।3.6.5।। सर्वे ब्राह्म्या श्रिया जुष्टा दृढयोगास्समाहिताः। शरभङ्गाश्रमे राममभिजग्मुश्च तापसाः।।3.6.6।।

vaikhānasā vālakhilyāssamprakṣālā marīcipāḥ| aśmakuṭṭāśca bahavḥ patrāhārāśca tāpasāḥ||3.6.2|| dantolūkhalinaścaiva tathaivonmajjakāḥ pare| gātraśayyā aśayyāśca tathaivābhrāvakāśakāḥ||3.6.3|| munayassalilāhārāvāyubhakṣā stathāpare| ākāśanilayāścaiva tathā sthaṇḍilaśāyinḥ||3.6.4|| vratopavāsino dāntāstathā'rdrapaṭavāsasḥ| sajapāśca taponityāstathā pañcatapo'nvitāḥ||3.6.5|| sarve brāhmyā śriyā juṣṭā dṛḍhayogāssamāhitāḥ| śarabhaṅgāśrame rāmamabhijagmuśca tāpasāḥ||3.6.6||

Vaikhanasas, Valakhilyas, sages who continuously wash their bodies, Marichakas Asmakuttas sages who live on leaves only, those who have teeth like mortar, Unmajjakas, those who use limbs as their bed, those who practise penance without using a bed, those who do penance in the open, unmindful of rain or Sun or wind, those who live on water only, those who penance under the open sky, those who carry on penance on high places like the mountaintop, those who recline on bare ground only, those who observe fast as a part of religious tradition, those selfrestrained men, those who mutter sacred mantras, those who constantly perform penance and those who stand under the blazing Sun in summer placing fire on four sides while performing penanceall these types of sages met Rama in the hermitage of Sarabhanga.

7Aranya Kanda 6.7

अभिगम्य च धर्मज्ञा रामं धर्मभृतां वरम्। ऊचुः परमधर्मज्ञमृषिसङ्घास्समाहिताः।।3.6.7।।

abhigamya ca dharmajñā rāmṃ dharmabhṛtāṃ varam| ūcuḥ paramadharmajñamṛṣisaṅghāssamāhitāḥ||3.6.7||

All groups of sages, who knew dharma, and were upholders of righteousness reached Rama, the supreme knower of dharma, and said:

8Aranya Kanda 6.8

त्वमिक्ष्वाकुकुलस्यास्य पृथिव्याश्च महारथ। प्रधानश्चासि नाथश्च देवानां मघवानिव।।3.6.8।।

tvamikṣvākukulasyāsya pṛthivyāśca mahāratha| pradhānaścāsi nāthaśca devānāṃ maghavāniva||3.6.8||

O great charioteer you are the lord of the Ikshvaku race and of the world just like Indra, chief among the gods.

9Aranya Kanda 6.9

विश्रुतस्त्रिषु लोकेषु यशसा विक्रमेण च। पितृभक्तिश्च सत्यं च त्वयि धर्मश्च पुष्कलः।।3.6.9।।

viśrutastriṣu lokeṣu yaśasā vikrameṇa ca| pitṛbhaktiśca satyṃ ca tvayi dharmaśca puṣkalḥ||3.6.9||

You are renowned in the three worlds for your valour, for your devotion to father, and in you lies enough evidence of your adherence to truth and righteousness.

10Aranya Kanda 6.10

त्वामासाद्य महात्मानं धर्मज्ञं धर्मवत्सलम्। अर्थित्वान्नाथ वक्ष्यामस्तच्च नः क्षन्तुमर्हसि।।3.6.10।।

tvāmāsādya mahātmānṃ dharmajñṃ dharmavatsalam| arthitvānnātha vakṣyāmastacca nḥ kṣantumarhasi||3.6.10||

O lord, do excuse us. We sought your help to fulfil our needs since you are at hand. You are a great soul, knower and lover of dharma.

11Aranya Kanda 6.11

अधर्मस्सुमहांस्तात भवेत्तस्य महीपतेः। यो हरेद्बलिषड्भागां न च रक्षति पुत्रवत्।।3.6.11।।

adharmassumahāṃstāta bhavettasya mahīpateḥ| yo haredbaliṣaḍbhāgāṃ na ca rakṣati putravat||3.6.11||

O Lord, a king who collect one sixth of the produce as tax and yet does not protect his subjects like his children commits an act of grave injustice.

12Aranya Kanda 6.12

युञ्जानस्स्वानिव प्राणान्प्राणैरिष्टान्सुतानिव। नित्ययुक्तस्सदा रक्षन्सर्वान्विषयवासिनः।।3.6.12।। प्राप्नोति शाश्वतीं राम कीर्तिं स बहुवार्षिकीम्। ब्रह्मणस्स्थानमासाद्य तत्र चापि महीयते।।3.6.13।।

yuñjānassvāniva prāṇānprāṇairiṣṭānsutāniva| nityayuktassadā rakṣansarvānviṣayavāsinḥ||3.6.12|| prāpnoti śāśvatīṃ rāma kīrtiṃ sa bahuvārṣikīm| brahmaṇassthānamāsādya tatra cāpi mahīyate||3.6.13||

O Rama, whoever protects the inhabitants of his kingdom at all times like his own son who he loves more than his own life will attain the world of Brahma and remain there for long years and will achieve prosperity thereafter.

14Aranya Kanda 6.14

यत्करोति परं धर्म मुनिर्मूलफलाशनः। तत्र राज्ञश्चतुर्भाग प्रजा धर्मेण रक्षितः।।3.6.14।।

yatkaroti parṃ dharma munirmūlaphalāśanḥ| tatra rājñaścaturbhāga prajā dharmeṇa rakṣitḥ||3.6.14||

One fourth of the supreme merit a sage earns by living on fruits and roots, accrues to the king through which the people are protected righteously.

15Aranya Kanda 6.15

सोऽयं ब्राह्मणभूयिष्ठो वानप्रस्थगणो महान्। त्वन्नाथोऽनाथवद्राम राक्षसैर्वध्यते भृशम्।।3.6.15।।

so'yṃ brāhmaṇabhūyiṣṭho vānaprasthagaṇo mahān| tvannātho'nāthavadrāma rākṣasairvadhyate bhṛśam||3.6.15||

O Rama we are a group of great sages, mostly brahmins, leading a vanaprastha life. Yet with a protector like you, we are slaughtered in large numbers like orphans.

16Aranya Kanda 6.16

एहि पश्य शरीराणि मुनीनां भावितात्मनाम्। हतानां राक्षसैर्घोरैर्बहूनां बहुधा वने।।3.6.16।।

ehi paśya śarīrāṇi munīnāṃ bhāvitātmanām| hatānāṃ rākṣasairghorairbahūnāṃ bahudhā vane||3.6.16||

Come and see the bodies of ascetics, who had perceived the Supreme Spirit, killed in large numbers in the forest by the fierce demons.

17Aranya Kanda 6.17

पम्पानदीनिवासानामनुमन्दाकिनीमपि। चित्रकूटालयानां च क्रियते कदनं महत्।।3.6.17।।

pampānadīnivāsānāmanumandākinīmapi| citrakūṭālayānāṃ ca kriyate kadanṃ mahat||3.6.17||

A great slaughter is taking place amongst those residing on the bank of the Pampa lake, near the river Mandakini, and on mount Chitrakuta.

18Aranya Kanda 6.18

एवं वयं न मृष्यामो विप्रकारं तपस्विनाम्। क्रियमाणं वने घोरं रक्षोभिर्भीमकर्मभिः।।3.6.18।।

evṃ vayṃ na mṛṣyāmo viprakārṃ tapasvinām| kriyamāṇṃ vane ghorṃ rakṣobhirbhīmakarmabhiḥ||3.6.18||

We ascetics are not able to tolerate the fearful deeds perpetrated by the dreadful demons in the forest.

19Aranya Kanda 6.19

ततस्त्वां शरणार्थं च शरण्यं समुपस्थिताः। परिपालय नो राम वध्यमानान्निशाचरैः।।3.6.19।।

tatastvāṃ śaraṇārthṃ ca śaraṇyṃ samupasthitāḥ| paripālaya no rāma vadhyamānānniśācaraiḥ||3.6.19||

O Rama, you are worthy of refuge. Hence we have come to you seeking your protection. We are being killed by the nightstalkers. Save us.

20Aranya Kanda 6.20

परा त्वत्तो गतिर्वीर पृथिव्यां नोपपद्यते। परिपालय न स्सर्वान्राक्षसेभ्यो नृपात्मज।।3.6.20।।

parā tvatto gatirvīra pṛthivyāṃ nopapadyate| paripālaya na ssarvānrākṣasebhyo nṛpātmaja||3.6.20||

O great warrior here on this earth there is no protector as mighty as you. So, O prince save us all from these demons.

21Aranya Kanda 6.21

एतच्छ्रुत्वा तु काकुत्स्थस्तापसानां तपस्विनाम्। इदं प्रोवाच धर्मात्मा सर्वानेव तपस्विनः।।3.6.21।।

etacchrutvā tu kākutsthastāpasānāṃ tapasvinām| idṃ provāca dharmātmā sarvāneva tapasvinḥ||3.6.21||

Having heard this, righteous Rama of the Kakutstha dynasty said to all the ascetics engaged in penance:

22Aranya Kanda 6.22

नैवमर्हथ मां वक्तुमाज्ञाप्योऽहं तपस्विनाम्। केवलेनात्मकार्येण प्रवेष्टव्यं मया वनं।।3.6.22।।

naivamarhatha māṃ vaktumājñāpyo'hṃ tapasvinām| kevalenātmakāryeṇa praveṣṭavyṃ mayā vanṃ||3.6.22||

You should not speak to me this way. I deserve to be commanded by the ascetics. I have not entered this forest only on a personal mission.

23Aranya Kanda 6.23

विप्रकारमपाक्रष्टुं राक्षसैर्भवतामिमम्। पितुस्तु निर्देशकरः प्रविष्टोऽहमिदं वनम्।।3.6.23।।

viprakāramapākraṣṭuṃ rākṣasairbhavatāmimam| pitustu nirdeśakarḥ praviṣṭo'hamidṃ vanam||3.6.23||

I came into this forest in obedience to my father's orders to repel the aggression of demons against you.

24Aranya Kanda 6.24

भवतामर्थसिद्ध्यर्थमागतोऽहं यदृच्छया। तस्य मेऽयं वने वासो भविष्यति महाफलः।।3.6.24।।

bhavatāmarthasiddhyarthamāgato'hṃ yadṛcchayā| tasya me'yṃ vane vāso bhaviṣyati mahāphalḥ||3.6.24||

I came here to acomplish your task incidentally, Therefore, my stay in the forest will yield great results.

25Aranya Kanda 6.25

तपस्विनां रणे शत्रून्हन्तुमिच्छामि राक्षसान्। पश्यन्तु वीर्यमृषयस्सभ्रातुर्मे तपोधनाः।।3.6.25।।

tapasvināṃ raṇe śatrūnhantumicchāmi rākṣasān| paśyantu vīryamṛṣayassabhrāturme tapodhanāḥ||3.6.25||

I wish to kill in the fight the demons who are your enemies. O great ascetics, you may witness my prowess supported by my brother.

26Aranya Kanda 6.26

दत्त्वाऽभयं चापि तपोधनानां धर्मे धृतात्मा सह लक्ष्मणेन। तपोधनैश्चापि सभाज्यवृत्तः सुतीक्ष्णमेवाभिजगाम वीरः।।3.6.26।।

dattvā'bhayṃ cāpi tapodhanānāṃ dharme dhṛtātmā saha lakṣmaṇena| tapodhanaiścāpi sabhājyavṛttḥ sutīkṣṇamevābhijagāma vīrḥ||3.6.26||

Brave Rama, steadfast in righteousness, worthy of honour, gave assurance to protect the ascetics endowed with the wealth of penance, and proceeded towards sage Sutikshna. इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीय आदिकाव्ये अरण्यकाण्डे षष्ठस्सर्गः।। Thus ends the sixth sarga of Aranyakanda of the holy Ramayana the first epic composed by sage Valmiki.
