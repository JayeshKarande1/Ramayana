Sarga 44

Valmiki Ramayana · Kishkindha Kanda · 17 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 44 / 67
1Kishkindha Kanda 44.1

विशेषेण तु सुग्रीवो हनूमत्यर्थमुक्तवान्। स हि तस्मिन्हरिश्रेष्ठे निश्चितार्थोऽर्थसाधने।।4.44.1।।

viśeṣeṇa tu sugrīvo hanūmatyarthamuktavān| sa hi tasminhariśreṣṭhe niścitārtho'rthasādhane||4.44.1||

Sugriva mentioned about the capacity of Hanuman, the best of monkeys as he knew he would surely succeed in accomplishing the task.

2Kishkindha Kanda 44.2

अब्रवीच्च हनूमन्तं विक्रान्तमनिलात्मजम्। सुग्रीवः परमप्रीतः प्रभु स्सर्ववनौकसाम्।।4.44.2।।

abravīcca hanūmantṃ vikrāntamanilātmajam| sugrīvḥ paramaprītḥ prabhu ssarvavanaukasām||4.44.2||

Sugriva, king of the monkeys, spoke, highly pleased, to Hanuman, the warrior son of the Windgod advancing towards him:

3Kishkindha Kanda 44.3

न भूमौ नान्तरिक्षे वा नाम्बरे नामरालये। नाप्सु वा गतिसङ्गं ते पश्यामि हरिपुङ्गव।।4.44.3।।

na bhūmau nāntarikṣe vā nāmbare nāmarālaye| nāpsu vā gatisaṅgṃ te paśyāmi haripuṅgava||4.44.3||

'O best of monkeys I scarcely see a hero comparable to your speed on earth or in space or sky or heaven or water.

4Kishkindha Kanda 44.4

सासुरास्सहगन्धर्वास्सनागनरदेवताः। विदिता स्सर्वलोकास्ते ससागरधराधराः।।4.44.4।।

sāsurāssahagandharvāssanāganaradevatāḥ| viditā ssarvalokāste sasāgaradharādharāḥ||4.44.4||

5Kishkindha Kanda 44.5

गतिर्वेगश्च तेजश्च लाघवं च महाकपे। पितुस्ते सदृशं वीर मारुतस्य महौजसः।।4.44.5।।

gatirvegaśca tejaśca lāghavṃ ca mahākape| pituste sadṛśṃ vīra mārutasya mahaujasḥ||4.44.5||

'O hero, O great monkey in movement, speed, brilliance and quickness you are similar to your mighty father, the Windgod.

6Kishkindha Kanda 44.6

तेजसा वापि ते भूतं न समं भुवि न विद्यते। तद्यथा लभ्यते सीता तत्त्वमेवोपपादय।।4.44.6।।

tejasā vāpi te bhūtṃ na samṃ bhuvi na vidyate| tadyathā labhyate sītā tattvamevopapādaya||4.44.6||

'I do not know any living being equal to you in brilliance on this earth. Therefore, you alone know how to secure Sita.Go ahead

7Kishkindha Kanda 44.7

त्वय्येव हनुम न्नस्ति बलं बुद्धिः पराक्रमः। देशकालानुवृत्तिश्च नयश्च नयपण्डित।।4.44.7।।

tvayyeva hanuma nnasti balṃ buddhiḥ parākramḥ| deśakālānuvṛttiśca nayaśca nayapaṇḍita||4.44.7||

'O learned Hanuman I am of opinion that in you alone rest strength, wisdom, prowess, ability to act in accordance with time and place and knowledge of statecraft'.

8Kishkindha Kanda 44.8

ततः कार्यसमासङ्गमवगम्य हनूमति। विदित्वा हनुमन्तं च चिन्तयामास राघवः।।4.44.8।।

tatḥ kāryasamāsaṅgamavagamya hanūmati| viditvā hanumantṃ ca cintayāmāsa rāghavḥ||4.44.8||

Having understood that Hanuman was capable of accomplishing the goal, Rama started reflecting as follows:

9Kishkindha Kanda 44.9

सर्वथा निश्चितार्थोऽयं हनूमति हरीश्वरः। निश्चितार्थकरश्चापि हनूमान्कार्यसाधने।।4.44.9।।

sarvathā niścitārtho'yṃ hanūmati harīśvarḥ| niścitārthakaraścāpi hanūmānkāryasādhane||4.44.9||

'This monkey king, Sugriva has confidence in Hanuman that he will by all means accomplish the task.'

10Kishkindha Kanda 44.10

तदेवं प्रस्थितस्यास्य परिज्ञातस्य कर्मभिः। भर्त्रा परिगृहीतस्य ध्रुवः कार्यफलोदयः।।4.44.10।।

tadevṃ prasthitasyāsya parijñātasya karmabhiḥ| bhartrā parigṛhītasya dhruvḥ kāryaphalodayḥ||4.44.10||

'The king Sugriva knows for sure that if Hanuman goes forth, success is assured. So he favours him.'

11Kishkindha Kanda 44.11

तं समीक्ष्य महातेजा व्यवसायोत्तरं हरिम्। कृतार्थ इव संवृत्तः प्रहृष्टेन्द्रियमानसः।।4.44.11।।

tṃ samīkṣya mahātejā vyavasāyottarṃ harim| kṛtārtha iva sṃvṛttḥ prahṛṣṭendriyamānasḥ||4.44.11||

The mighty Rama was happy in his heart to see Hanuman. He felt as if the task is achieved.

12Kishkindha Kanda 44.12

ददौ तस्य ततः प्रीतस्स्वनामाङ्कोपशोभितम्। अङ्गुलीयमभिज्ञानं राजपुत्र्याः परन्तपः।।4.44.12।।

dadau tasya tatḥ prītassvanāmāṅkopaśobhitam| aṅgulīyamabhijñānṃ rājaputryāḥ parantapḥ||4.44.12||

Rama, scorcher of enemies, gladly gave Hanuman a ring inscribed with his name as a mark of identification by princess (Sita). And said:

13Kishkindha Kanda 44.13

अनेन त्वां हरिश्रेष्ठ चिह्नेन जनकात्मजा। मत्सकाशादनुप्राप्तमनुद्विग्नाऽनुपश्यति।।4.44.13।।

anena tvāṃ hariśreṣṭha cihnena janakātmajā| matsakāśādanuprāptamanudvignā'nupaśyati||4.44.13||

'O distinguished monkey Janaka's daughter will greet you without any hesitation when you present this signet to her. She will know that you have come from me.

14Kishkindha Kanda 44.14

व्यवसायश्च ते वीर सत्त्वयुक्तश्च विक्रमः। सुग्रीवस्य च सन्देशस्सिद्धिं कथयतीव मे।।4.44.14।।

vyavasāyaśca te vīra sattvayuktaśca vikramḥ| sugrīvasya ca sandeśassiddhiṃ kathayatīva me||4.44.14||

'O great hero your enterprise, prowess coupled with courage and Sugriva's message to you seem to indicate to me about the success of your mission'.

15Kishkindha Kanda 44.15

स तद्गृह्य हरिश्रेष्ठः स्थाप्य मूर्ध्नि कृताञ्जलिः। वन्दित्वा चरणौ चैव प्रस्थितः प्लवगोत्तमः।।4.44.15।।

sa tadgṛhya hariśreṣṭhḥ sthāpya mūrdhni kṛtāñjaliḥ| vanditvā caraṇau caiva prasthitḥ plavagottamḥ||4.44.15||

The foremost among the monkeys took the ring given to him, placed it on his forehead and saluated it with folded palms. He then prostrated at the feet of Rama and departed.

16Kishkindha Kanda 44.16

स तत्प्रकर्षन् हरीणां महद्बलं बभूव वीरः पवनात्मजः कपिः। गताम्बुदे व्योम्नि विशुद्धमण्डलः शशीव नक्षत्रगणोपशोभितः।।4.44.16।।

sa tatprakarṣan harīṇāṃ mahadbalṃ babhūva vīrḥ pavanātmajḥ kapiḥ| gatāmbude vyomni viśuddhamaṇḍalḥ śaśīva nakṣatragaṇopaśobhitḥ||4.44.16||

Like the moon adorned by hosts of stars in the cloudless sky, the mighty heroic Hanuman, the great son of the Windgod, shone in the midst of the army of monkeys.

17Kishkindha Kanda 44.17

अतिबल बलमाश्रितस्तवाहं हरिवरविक्रम विक्रमैरनल्पैः। पवनसुत यथाऽभिगम्यते सा जनकसुता हनुमं स्तथा कुरुष्व।।4.44.17।।

atibala balamāśritastavāhṃ harivaravikrama vikramairanalpaiḥ| pavanasuta yathā'bhigamyate sā janakasutā hanumṃ stathā kuruṣva||4.44.17||

O Hanuman son of the Windgod, O valiant among the Vanaras, I bank on your strength. Do whatever you can with your mighty valour so that the daughter of Janaka can be found. इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीय आदिकाव्ये किष्किन्धाकाण्डे चतुश्चत्वारिंशस्सर्गः।। Thus ends the fortyfourth sarga in Kishkindakanda of the first epic, the Holy Ramayana composed by sage Valmiki.
