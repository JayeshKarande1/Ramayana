Sarga 58

Valmiki Ramayana · Kishkindha Kanda · 31 Sacred Shlokas

Sarga 58 / 67
1Kishkindha Kanda 58.1

इत्युक्तः करुणं वाक्यं वानरैस्त्यक्तजीवितैः। सबाष्पो वानरान्गृध्रः प्रत्युवाच महास्वनः।।4.58.1।।

ityuktḥ karuṇṃ vākyṃ vānaraistyaktajīvitaiḥ| sabāṣpo vānarāngṛdhrḥ pratyuvāca mahāsvanḥ||4.58.1||

On hearing the pathetic words from the monkeys who had given up hope of life, the loudvoiced vulture wailing replied:

2Kishkindha Kanda 58.2

यवीयान्मम भ्राता जटायुर्नाम वानराः। यमाख्यात हतं युद्धे रावणेन बलीयसा।।4.58.2।।

yavīyānmama bhrātā jaṭāyurnāma vānarāḥ| yamākhyāta hatṃ yuddhe rāvaṇena balīyasā||4.58.2||

'O monkeys, the one you said to have been killed by mighty Ravana in a combat was my younger brother, Jatayu.

3Kishkindha Kanda 58.3

वृद्धभावादपक्षत्वाच्छृण्वंस्तदपि मर्षये। न हि मे शक्तिरस्त्यद्य भ्रातुर्वैरविमोक्षणे।।4.58.3।।

vṛddhabhāvādapakṣatvācchṛṇvṃstadapi marṣaye| na hi me śaktirastyadya bhrāturvairavimokṣaṇe||4.58.3||

'Even after I have heard this, I cannot avenge my brother's death as I am old, I have no wings, and I have no energy enough (to fight).

4Kishkindha Kanda 58.4

पुरा वृत्रवधे वृत्ते परस्परजयैषिणौ। आदित्यमुपयातौ स्वो ज्वलन्तं रश्मिमालिनम्।।4.58.4।।

purā vṛtravadhe vṛtte parasparajayaiṣiṇau| ādityamupayātau svo jvalantṃ raśmimālinam||4.58.4||

'In olden days, when Indra killed Vrutra we (Jatayu and I) seeking to challenge him reached the Sun blazing with a garland of beams.

5Kishkindha Kanda 58.5

आवृत्त्याऽकाशमार्गे तु जवेन स्म गतौ भृशम्। मध्यं प्राप्ते दिनकरे जटायुरवसीदति।।4.58.5।।

āvṛttyā'kāśamārge tu javena sma gatau bhṛśam| madhyṃ prāpte dinakare jaṭāyuravasīdati||4.58.5||

'Flying in the sky at a violently high speed, we reached the meridian of the Sun when, unable to bear the heat, Jatayu became weak.

6Kishkindha Kanda 58.6

तमहं भ्रातरं दृष्ट्वा सूर्यरश्मिभिरर्दितम्। पक्षाभ्यां छादयामास स्नेहात्परमविह्वलः।।4.58.6।।

tamahṃ bhrātarṃ dṛṣṭvā sūryaraśmibhirarditam| pakṣābhyāṃ chādayāmāsa snehātparamavihvalḥ||4.58.6||

'Seeing my brother tormented due to the Sun's radiance I was overwhelmed with grief. Out of love I covered him with both my wings.

7Kishkindha Kanda 58.7

निर्दग्धपक्षः पतितो विन्ध्येऽहं वानरर्षभाः। अहमस्मिन्वसन्भ्रातुः प्रवृत्तिं नोपलक्षये।।4.58.7।।

nirdagdhapakṣḥ patito vindhye'hṃ vānararṣabhāḥ| ahamasminvasanbhrātuḥ pravṛttiṃ nopalakṣaye||4.58.7||

'O bulls among monkeys, consequently my wings were burnt and I dropped down on Vindhya. Here I have been living, knowing not my brother's whereabouts.'

8Kishkindha Kanda 58.8

जटायुषस्त्वेवमुक्तो भ्रात्रा सम्पातिना तदा। युवराजो महाप्राज्ञः प्रत्युवाचाङ्गदस्ततः।।4.58.8।।

jaṭāyuṣastvevamukto bhrātrā sampātinā tadā| yuvarājo mahāprājñḥ pratyuvācāṅgadastatḥ||4.58.8||

To these words of Jatayu's brother, Sampati, Angada, the learned heir apparent replied:

9Kishkindha Kanda 58.9

जटायुषो यदि भ्राता श्रुतं ते गदितं मया। आख्या हि यदि जानासि निलयं तस्य रक्षसः।।4.58.9।।

jaṭāyuṣo yadi bhrātā śrutṃ te gaditṃ mayā| ākhyā hi yadi jānāsi nilayṃ tasya rakṣasḥ||4.58.9||

'I have heard you. If you are the brother of Jatayu, tell us about the abode of that demon if you know.

10Kishkindha Kanda 58.10

अदीर्घदर्शिनं तं वै रावणं राक्षसाधिपम्। अन्तिके यदि वा दूरे यदि जानासि शंस नः।।4.58.10।।

adīrghadarśinṃ tṃ vai rāvaṇṃ rākṣasādhipam| antike yadi vā dūre yadi jānāsi śṃsa nḥ||4.58.10||

'Tell us about Ravana, king of the demons who is unaware of the consequences. If you know his residence, tell us if it is near or far.'

11Kishkindha Kanda 58.11

ततोऽब्रवीन्महातेजा ज्येष्ठो भ्राता जटायुषः। आत्मानुरूपं वचनं वानरान्सम्प्रहर्षयन्।।4.58.11।।

tato'bravīnmahātejā jyeṣṭho bhrātā jaṭāyuṣḥ| ātmānurūpṃ vacanṃ vānarānsampraharṣayan||4.58.11||

Then Jatayu's elder brother, the glorious Sampati, said these pleasing, befitting words:

12Kishkindha Kanda 58.12

निर्दग्धपक्षो गृध्रोऽहं हीनवीर्य: प्लवङ्गमाः। वाङ्मात्रेण तु रामस्य करिष्ये साह्यमुत्तमम्।।4.58.12।।

nirdagdhapakṣo gṛdhro'hṃ hīnavīrya: plavaṅgamāḥ| vāṅmātreṇa tu rāmasya kariṣye sāhyamuttamam||4.58.12||

'O monkeys I am a vulture. My wings are burnt and my strength is depleted. The only substantial help I can render Rama is through my words.

13Kishkindha Kanda 58.13

जानामि वारुणान्लोकान्विष्णोस्त्रै विक्रमानपि। महासुरविमर्दन्वा श्च ह्यमृतस्य च मन्थनम्।।4.58.13।।

jānāmi vāruṇānlokānviṣṇostrai vikramānapi| mahāsuravimardanvā śca hyamṛtasya ca manthanam||4.58.13||

'I know Varuna's worlds(seas and oceans) and all the three worlds measured by three strides by Vamana. Indeed I also know about the friction between the great gods and demons and the churning of the ocean for nectar.

14Kishkindha Kanda 58.14

रामस्य यदिदं कार्यं कर्तव्यं प्रथमं मया। जरया च हृतं तेजः प्राणाश्च शिथिला मम।।4.58.14।।

rāmasya yadidṃ kāryṃ kartavyṃ prathamṃ mayā| jarayā ca hṛtṃ tejḥ prāṇāśca śithilā mama||4.58.14||

'Rama's work is my primary duty. (But) my strength is drained by age and I have lost my vitality.

15Kishkindha Kanda 58.15

तरुणी रूपसम्पन्ना सर्वाभरणभूषिता। ह्रियमाणा मया दृष्टा रावणेन दुरात्मना।।4.58.15।। क्रोशन्ती राम रामेति लक्ष्मणेति च भामिनी। भूषणान्यपविध्यन्ती गात्राणि च विधुन्वती।।4.58.16।।

taruṇī rūpasampannā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitā| hriyamāṇā mayā dṛṣṭā rāvaṇena durātmanā||4.58.15|| krośantī rāma rāmeti lakṣmaṇeti ca bhāminī| bhūṣaṇānyapavidhyantī gātrāṇi ca vidhunvatī||4.58.16||

'I saw a young lady of beautiful appearance, adorned with all kinds of ornaments borne away by the evilminded Ravana. That dainty lady was calling out, 'O Rama, O Lakshmana' and was crying aloud. She was throwing away her ornaments and was wriggling to get out of Ravana's grip.

17Kishkindha Kanda 58.17

सूर्यप्रभेव शैलाग्रे तस्याः कौशेयमुत्तमम्। असिते राक्षसे भाति यथा वा तटिदम्बुदे।।4.58.17।।

sūryaprabheva śailāgre tasyāḥ kauśeyamuttamam| asite rākṣase bhāti yathā vā taṭidambude||4.58.17||

'Her fine silk garment was shining like the radiance of the Sun on the mountain peak, like lightning against the dark cloud. (That was Ravana).

18Kishkindha Kanda 58.18

तां तु सीतामहं मन्ये रामस्य परिकीर्तनात्। श्रूयतां मे कथयतो निलयं तस्य रक्षसः।।4.58.18।।

tāṃ tu sītāmahṃ manye rāmasya parikīrtanāt| śrūyatāṃ me kathayato nilayṃ tasya rakṣasḥ||4.58.18||

'Since she was uttering Rama's name I think she is Sita. (Now) I will tell you about the demon's abode. Listen

19Kishkindha Kanda 58.19

पुत्रो विश्रवस स्साक्षाद्भ्राता वैश्रवणस्य च। अध्यास्ते नगरीं लङ्कां रावणो नाम राक्षसः।।4.58.19।।

putro viśravasa ssākṣādbhrātā vaiśravaṇasya ca| adhyāste nagarīṃ laṅkāṃ rāvaṇo nāma rākṣasḥ||4.58.19||

'He is Vishrava's son and halfbrother of Vaishravana. His name is Ravana and he resides in the city of Lanka.

20Kishkindha Kanda 58.20

इतो द्वीपस्समुद्रस्य सम्पूर्णे शतयोजने। तस्मिन्लङ्कापुरी रम्या निर्मिता विश्वकर्मणा।।4.58.20।।

ito dvīpassamudrasya sampūrṇe śatayojane| tasminlaṅkāpurī ramyā nirmitā viśvakarmaṇā||4.58.20||

'The beautiful city of Lanka is an island, a hundred yojanas away from here.It was built by Visvakarma.

21Kishkindha Kanda 58.21

जाम्बूनदमयैर्द्वारैश्चित्रैः काञ्चनवेदिकैः। प्रासादैर्हेमवर्णैश्च महद्भिः सुसमा कृता।।4.58.21।। प्राकारेणार्कवर्णेन महता च समान्विता।

jāmbūnadamayairdvāraiścitraiḥ kāñcanavedikaiḥ| prāsādairhemavarṇaiśca mahadbhiḥ susamā kṛtā||4.58.21|| prākāreṇārkavarṇena mahatā ca samānvitā|

'The city has golden gates, golden platforms, huge, goldencoloured, uniformly built mansions surrounded by a tall boundary wall of the colour of sunlight.

22Kishkindha Kanda 58.22

तस्यां वसति वैदेही दीना कौशेयवासिनी।।4.58.22।। रावणान्तःपुरे रुद्धा राक्षसीभिस्सुरक्षिता। जनकस्यात्मजां राज्ञस्तत्र द्रक्ष्यथ मैथिलीम्।।4.58.23।।

tasyāṃ vasati vaidehī dīnā kauśeyavāsinī||4.58.22|| rāvaṇāntḥpure ruddhā rākṣasībhissurakṣitā| janakasyātmajāṃ rājñastatra drakṣyatha maithilīm||4.58.23||

'You will see in the city of Lanka, the princess from Videha, daughter of Janaka, a picture of dejection, robed in silk, confined in the harem of Ravana, wellprotected by demonesses.

24Kishkindha Kanda 58.24

लङ्कायामथ गुप्तायां सागरेण समन्ततः। सम्प्राप्य सागरस्यान्तं सम्पूर्णं शतयोजनम्।।4.58.24।। आसाद्य दक्षिणं तीरं ततो द्रक्ष्यथ रावणम्। तत्रैव त्वरिताः क्षिप्रं विक्रमध्वं प्लवङ्गमाः।।4.58.25।।

laṅkāyāmatha guptāyāṃ sāgareṇa samantatḥ| samprāpya sāgarasyāntṃ sampūrṇṃ śatayojanam||4.58.24|| āsādya dakṣiṇṃ tīrṃ tato drakṣyatha rāvaṇam| tatraiva tvaritāḥ kṣiprṃ vikramadhvṃ plavaṅgamāḥ||4.58.25||

'O monkeys traverse a hundred yojanas across the sea. On reaching the southern coast you will be able to see Ravana's Lanka defended and surrounded by the sea. Now proceed swiftly and prove your strength.

26Kishkindha Kanda 58.26

ज्ञानेन खलु पश्यामि दृष्ट्वा प्रत्यागमिष्यथ। आद्यः पन्थाः कुलिङ्गानां ये चान्ये धान्यजीविनः।।4.58.26।। द्वितीयो बलिभोजानां ये च वृक्षफलाशिनः। भासास्तृतीयं गच्छन्ति क्रौञ्चाश्च कुररैस्सह।।4.58.27।। श्येनाश्चतुर्थं गच्छन्ति गृध्रा गच्छन्ति पञ्चमम्।

jñānena khalu paśyāmi dṛṣṭvā pratyāgamiṣyatha| ādyḥ panthāḥ kuliṅgānāṃ ye cānye dhānyajīvinḥ||4.58.26|| dvitīyo balibhojānāṃ ye ca vṛkṣaphalāśinḥ| bhāsāstṛtīyṃ gacchanti krauñcāśca kuraraissaha||4.58.27|| śyenāścaturthṃ gacchanti gṛdhrā gacchanti pañcamam|

'By intuition I know you would see Sita and return. On the first plane you will see sparrows and birds which live on grain. On the second, you will see crows living on the food offered to the dead and birds living on the fruits of trees, and then cocks, cranes and kraunchas moving on the third plane, on the fourth, hawks and falcons and on the fifth, vultures.

28Kishkindha Kanda 58.28

बलवीर्योपपन्नानां रूपयौवनशालिनाम्।।4.58.28।। षष्ठस्तु पन्था हंसानां वैनतेयगतिः परा। वैनतेयाच्च नो जन्म सर्वेषां वानरर्षभाः।।4.58.29।।

balavīryopapannānāṃ rūpayauvanaśālinām||4.58.28|| ṣaṣṭhastu panthā hṃsānāṃ vainateyagatiḥ parā| vainateyācca no janma sarveṣāṃ vānararṣabhāḥ||4.58.29||

'O bulls among monkeys swans blessed with strength, vigour, beauty and youth move in the sixth region. Beyond, on the seventh plane, you will see the descendants of Garuda.

30Kishkindha Kanda 58.30

इहस्थोऽहं प्रपश्यामि रावणं जानकीं तथा। अस्माकमपि सौवर्णं दिव्यं चक्षुर्बलं तथा।।4.58.30।।

ihastho'hṃ prapaśyāmi rāvaṇṃ jānakīṃ tathā| asmākamapi sauvarṇṃ divyṃ cakṣurbalṃ tathā||4.58.30||

'From here I see Ravana and Janaki. We (vultures) have a divine golden vision and great power.

31Kishkindha Kanda 58.31

तस्मादाहारवीर्येण निसर्गेण च वानराः। आयोजनशतात्साग्राद्वयं पश्याम नित्यशः।।4.58.31।।

tasmādāhāravīryeṇa nisargeṇa ca vānarāḥ| āyojanaśatātsāgrādvayṃ paśyāma nityaśḥ||4.58.31||

'O monkeys therefore, by the strength of food and by nature we can always see beyond a hundred yojanas from this edge.

32Kishkindha Kanda 58.32

अस्माकं विहिता वृत्तिर्निसर्गेण च दूरतः। विहिता पादमूले तु वृत्तिश्चरणयोधिनाम्।।4.58.32।।

asmākṃ vihitā vṛttirnisargeṇa ca dūratḥ| vihitā pādamūle tu vṛttiścaraṇayodhinām||4.58.32||

'Our movement and vision naturally extends to distant places. For those fighting on foot, the movement is restricted to the extent of foot movement.

33Kishkindha Kanda 58.33

गर्हितं तु कृतं कर्म येन स्म पशिताशिना। प्रतीकार्यं च मे तस्य वैरं भ्रातृकृतं भवेत्।।4.58.33।।

garhitṃ tu kṛtṃ karma yena sma paśitāśinā| pratīkāryṃ ca me tasya vairṃ bhrātṛkṛtṃ bhavet||4.58.33||

34Kishkindha Kanda 58.34

उपायो दृश्यतां कश्चिल्लङ्घने लवणाम्भसः। अभिगम्य तु वैदेहीं समृद्धार्था गमिष्यथ।।4.58.34।।

upāyo dṛśyatāṃ kaścillaṅghane lavaṇāmbhasḥ| abhigamya tu vaidehīṃ samṛddhārthā gamiṣyatha||4.58.34||

'Look out for some means to cross the salty ocean. You will see Vaidehi, and return, your mission accomplished.

35Kishkindha Kanda 58.35

समुद्रं नेतुमिच्छामि भवद्भिर्वरुणालयम्। प्रदास्याम्युदकं भ्रातुस्स्वर्गतस्य महात्मनः।।4.58.35।।

samudrṃ netumicchāmi bhavadbhirvaruṇālayam| pradāsyāmyudakṃ bhrātussvargatasya mahātmanḥ||4.58.35||

'Take me to the ocean, the abode of Varuna. I intend to offer oblation to my brother, a great soul who is in heaven.'

36Kishkindha Kanda 58.36

ततो नीत्वा तु तं देशं तीरं नदनदीपतेः। निर्दग्धपक्षं सम्पातिं वानरास्सुमहौजसः।।4.58.36।। पुनः प्रत्यानयित्वा च तं देशं पतगेश्वरम्। बभूवुर्वानरा हृष्टाः प्रवृत्तिमुपलभ्य ते।।4.58.37।।

tato nītvā tu tṃ deśṃ tīrṃ nadanadīpateḥ| nirdagdhapakṣṃ sampātiṃ vānarāssumahaujasḥ||4.58.36|| punḥ pratyānayitvā ca tṃ deśṃ patageśvaram| babhūvurvānarā hṛṣṭāḥ pravṛttimupalabhya te||4.58.37||

Th mightiest of monkeys carried Sampati, whose wings were totally burnt, to the sea shore and after he had offered oblations took him back to the same place. Having obtained all information from the king of vultures the monkeys were very happy. इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीय आदिकाव्ये किष्किन्धाकाण्डे अष्टपञ्चशस्सर्गः।। Thus ends the fiftyeighth sarga in Kishkindakanda of the first epic, the Holy Ramayana composed by sage Valmiki.
